This tutorial shows you how to place two videos side by side in Canva so they play together in one split-screen video.
Create a video design in the aspect ratio you need. For a standard landscape video, use a 16 by 9 layout.
Open elements and find two rectangular frames or use a two-column grid.
Position the containers so one fills the left half of the page and the other fills the right half.
Drag the first video into the left frame and the second into the right frame.
Double-click each video to adjust its crop and keep the important subject visible.
In the timeline, align the two video segments so they start together.
In Canva's updated editor, keep the second video on an upper track so it overlaps the first rather than playing after it.
Trim both clips to the intended duration.
If one video is shorter, trim the complete scene to that length or deliberately add another shot for the remaining time.
Adjust the audio separately.
For an interview, keep the relevant speaker audio.
For a visual comparison, mute both clips and use one music or narration track.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Placing two videos side by side on the canvas does not guarantee that their timeline segments overlap.
If they appear one after the other in time, the result will not play as a simultaneous split-screen.
Preview the opening, middle and ending to check the overlap and crops.
Add a narrow divider or labels if they help explain the comparison.
Download the result as mp4 and play the saved file to confirm that both sides stay visible for the full scene.
