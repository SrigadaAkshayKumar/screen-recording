This tutorial shows you how to find Canva templates you can customize and download with a free account.
Open Canva and select Templates, or visit Canva's main templates page.
Search for the design you need, such as a presentation, flyer, Instagram post or resume.
Make the search more useful by adding a style or purpose.
For example, search for a minimalist business presentation instead of just presentation.
Browse the results and check the template badges.
Premium templates are marked with a crown.
Choose a template without the premium badge, then open it in the editor.
Replace the sample text with your own information.
Upload your photos or choose free elements from the media library.
Keep an eye on the crown markers when adding new photos, graphics or other assets.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Starting with a free template does not guarantee that every item you add later will also be free.
A premium photo or graphic can introduce a payment requirement or watermark at download.
Before finishing, choose Share and Download, select the format you need and review any paid content notice.
If Canva identifies premium items, replace them with your own uploads or free alternatives and check again.
For a static graphic, PNG or JPG is usually suitable.
Use PDF for a document or printable layout and MP4 for a video design.
Keep the customized design in your account and duplicate it for your next project.
You can now use Canva's free templates without accidentally building your design around premium assets.
