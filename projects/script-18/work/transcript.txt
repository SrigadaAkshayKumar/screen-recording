This tutorial shows you the basic steps for editing a video in Canva's video timeline.
Create a video design and choose the format for your finished video.
Open Uploads, click Upload Files and select your footage.
Drag the first video onto the main timeline track.
Add your other clips after it in the order you want them to play.
Select a clip and drag its left or right edge inward to trim unwanted footage from the beginning or end.
To remove a section from the middle, move the playhead to the beginning of that section.
Select the clip, open More and choose Split.
Make another split at the section's end, then delete the unwanted middle segment.
Reorder clips by dragging them along the main track.
Preview each cut to check that the sequence still makes sense.
Open text and add a heading.
Set its font and position on the canvas, then adjust its timeline layer so it appears over the intended clip.
Add music or an uploaded voiceover on an audio track.
Select the audio to adjust its volume, then listen to make sure speech remains clear.
Crop or reposition the footage on the canvas when needed.
Add a simple transition between clips if it helps the change of scene.
