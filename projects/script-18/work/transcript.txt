This tutorial shows you what to check when audio keeps playing after you try to pause a Canva video.
First, click the timeline's pause control and watch the playhead.
If you use the spacebar, click outside any text box first so the key controls playback instead of entering text.
If the playhead stops but you still hear audio, check the audio panel.
A music sample you previewed in the library may be playing separately from the design.
Stop that preview and test the timeline again.
Also check for another open Canva tab or a separate presentation preview.
Pause those players so you are testing one source of sound.
If the design's own audio continues while its playhead is stopped,
wait for your changes to save then reload the editor.
Start and pause playback once before making further edits.
Try the same project in an updated browser.
A private window with extensions disabled can help you check whether the problem is specific to your normal browser session.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Hearing audio after pausing does not prove the audio clip is incorrectly placed on the timeline.
Confirm where the sound is coming from before deleting or replacing recordings.
If the issue persists, export a short MP4 and test its playback separately.
If the file behaves normally, report the editor playback problem to Canva Support with your browser details and the steps that reproduce it.
