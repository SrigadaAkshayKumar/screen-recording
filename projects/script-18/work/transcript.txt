This tutorial shows you how to create a flyer in Canva that explains an offer clearly and tells people what to do next.
Search Canva for flyer and choose the intended paper size.
For a smaller handout, start with an A5 design or create a custom size before you begin.
Pick a template that matches your purpose such as a class, sale, local service or community event.
Replace its main headline with a short statement of the offer.
For this example, use Weekend Beginners class.
Add the date, location, price and one sentence explaining who the class is for.
Upload a relevant photo and place it in the main image area.
Adjust the crop so the subject stays clear, then change the template colours to match your branding.
Make the next action obvious, add a contact number, website or booking instruction in a clear section near the bottom.
If you use a QR code, include a short label telling people what they will find after scanning it.
Align the text blocks and leave empty space between the headline, description and booking details.
Use a small number of fonts and avoid shrinking the important information to fit extra decoration.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Ask someone to look at the flyer for 5 seconds, then tell you what is being offered and how to respond.
If either answer is unclear, simplify the wording or make the action more prominent.
Check the spelling and contact details, then download PDF print for handouts.
Follow the printer's bleed requirements.
For online sharing, download a PNG and include the booking link in the accompanying post.
Your flyer is now ready for its intended audience and format.
