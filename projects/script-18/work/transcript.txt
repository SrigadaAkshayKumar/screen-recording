This tutorial shows you what to check when Canva says it cannot open a design.
First, open Canva's homepage and try a different design.
This helps you work out whether the problem affects one file or Canva more generally.
If only one design fails, check which account you are signed into.
A design shared with your school or work email may not open from your personal account.
Switch to the correct team or workspace and try opening the design from projects.
If someone sent you a link, ask them to send a fresh link and check that your exact account has access.
If the design belongs to you and has disappeared, check trash in Canva.
Restore it if that option is available.
For someone else's design, the owner needs to check whether it was deleted or its sharing settings changed.
If several designs fail, check your internet connection and Canva's official status page.
Close and reopen Canva, update your browser or app, and try another supported browser.
You can also test a private browser window.
If the design opens there, try disabling extensions in your normal browser, especially tools that block website scripts.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Clearing the cache cannot restore permission to a private design or recover a permanently deleted file.
Check the account, link, and ownership before spending time on browser fixes.
If you clear Canva's site data, first make sure any other open work has saved.
You may need to sign in again afterward.
If the problem continues, contact Canva support with the design link, exact error message, and the checks you have already tried.
