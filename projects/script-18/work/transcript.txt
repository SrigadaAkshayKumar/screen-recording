This tutorial shows you how to organize your Canva projects using clear names, folders, and quick access shortcuts.
Open projects from the homepage.
Find the first design you want to organize and rename it using its title beside the pencil icon.
Use a consistent pattern such as the app name, tutorial topic, and version.
For example, Canva Upload Audio Thumbnail Final tells you much more than copy of untitled design.
Create a folder for the project or tutorial batch.
Select the related designs using their thumbnail checkboxes, choose move, select the folder, and confirm.
Put source images and other assets in a nearby subfolder so you can find the materials used for that project.
For a design you use regularly, open its three-dot menu and choose star for you.
The start item becomes available from your start in the sidebar.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Recent designs is a history of what you opened rather than a complete organizational system.
A project becomes much easier to retrieve when its name and folder both explain what it contains.
Keep one clear working version and label any alternate versions deliberately.
Move completed work into a finished or archived folder that you create so active work remains easy to see.
Finally, search for one organized design by its topic to check that your naming system works.
Your Canva projects are now easier to browse and retrieve.
