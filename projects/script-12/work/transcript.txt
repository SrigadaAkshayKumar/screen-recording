This tutorial shows you how to upload a PDF to Canva and open it as a design you can work with.
From Canva's homepage, open Projects and click Add New, then choose Upload Files.
Select your PDF and click Open.
You can also drag the PDF directly onto Canva's homepage.
Wait for Canva to finish importing the document.
Open the imported design when it becomes available.
Check the page count and scroll through the document.
Compare the pages with your original PDF before making changes.
Click a text area.
If Canva has recognised it as an editable text box,
you can change the wording, font, size or alignment.
You can also select supported images and other imported elements to adjust them.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Importing a PDF does not guarantee that every object becomes editable.
A scanned page or flattened document may arrive as one image,
so its original words cannot be edited like normal Canva text.
If the text is flattened, add a new text box or recreate the part you need to change.
Check for missing objects, substituted fonts or shifted spacing before exporting.
When you are finished, select Share and Download.
Choose PDF Standard for a regular document or PDF print
when you need a higher quality print-oriented export.
Download the result and check the finished pages in a PDF viewer.
