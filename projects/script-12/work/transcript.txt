This tutorial shows you how to correct generated captions in Canva
and adjust a line that appears at the wrong time.
Open your video project and play the section containing the mistake.
Pause so you can compare the spoken phrase with the words on screen.
Select the caption segment on the timeline and type the correction.
You can also open captions from the toolbar,
select the matching line in the captions panel and edit it there.
Correct the wording without adding information the speaker did not say.
Check spelling, punctuation and numbers,
especially when a small mistake could change the instruction.
If the words are correct but appear too early or too late,
select that caption segment on the timeline.
Drag its start or end edge to match the spoken phrase,
then replay the surrounding section.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Changing the words does not automatically solve a timing problem.
A corrected caption can still disappear before the speaker finishes
or remain visible during the next sentence.
Listen to the phrase from a few seconds before it starts.
That makes it easier to judge the captioning context
rather than looking at one paused frame.
Continue through the other lines and preview the complete video.
When everything matches, download a new MP4
so the exported version contains your corrections.
