This tutorial shows you how to control when an animation begins and how long its object stays visible in Canva.
Open a video design and select the animated text, image or shape you want to adjust.
In the video timeline, find that object's layer.
Drag the whole layer left or right to change its starting time without changing its length.
For example, move a heading so it begins two seconds into the video.
Drag the right edge of the layer to change when the object disappears.
If it starts at two seconds and ends at six seconds, the object has a four-second visible span.
To change the entrance animation itself, select the object and open animate.
For supported effects, use the intro or outro duration controls.
Try a one-second intro then preview the movement from just before the object's starting time.
If the selected effect only provides speed presets, use those or choose an effect that offers the duration controls you need.
For a presentation or social post, right-click the object and use Show Element Timing when available.
For video designs, use the video timeline instead.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The object's visible span, the animation's intro duration and the total page duration are separate settings.
Making a page longer does not automatically delay an object's entrance.
Preview the full sequence and leave enough time after the entrance for viewers to read or inspect the object.
