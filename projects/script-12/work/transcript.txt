This tutorial shows you how to connect a domain you already own to a Canva website.
First, make sure you are editing a website design and that your Canva plan supports connecting an existing domain.
You also need access to the domain settings at the company where it is registered.
Open Publish Website and choose Get a New Domain.
Select Bring Your Own Domain, then Continue.
Enter the domain without the www.prefix.
You can also use a subdomain if the main address already hosts another website.
Continue to the connection step.
If Canva offers automatic connection for your provider, choose Login and Auto-Connect, then follow the provider's instructions.
For manual connection, open your domain provider's DNS management page.
Copy each record shown by Canva into the matching record type, name and value fields.
Use the exact values from your own Canva connection panel.
Change only the records needed for this connection.
Keep unrelated records, such as those used by your email service, in place.
Return to Canva and continue verification.
DNS updates can take up to 72 hours to finish, so a pending status immediately after saving does not necessarily mean the setup failed.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Connecting the domain and publishing the website are separate steps.
After Canva verifies the domain, select it as the site's address and complete publishing.
If verification fails, compare Canva's expected records with the current records at your provider
and correct conflicting entries for the address you are connecting.
Open the custom address in a private browser window and confirm that the correct website loads.
