This tutorial shows you how to move text around a stationary object in Canva using a custom animation path.
Create a video design and add your object near the centre of the canvas.
A product cut-out or a simple graphic works well because the surrounding space is easy to see.
Add a short text box, such as New Collection, and position it above the object.
Use a readable font and leave enough room for the text to travel without hitting the edge of the design.
If you want the words arranged in an arc, select the text, Open Effects, and Apply Curve.
Adjust the curve until the phrase has the shape you want.
Keep the text selected, click Animate and choose Create an Animation.
Drag the text around the object in a smooth circular or oval route, finishing close to where you started.
Release the mouse and preview the path.
Adjust the available movement settings and redraw the route if it has a sudden jump.
Click Done when the movement looks smooth.
Use the video timeline to give the scene enough time for the motion to play.
Keep the central object visible for the entire sequence.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Curve changes the shape of the text, while the custom path moves the entire text box.
The letters do not automatically rotate to face along the path,
and Canva does not turn this into a true, three-dimensional orbit.
For a clean result, keep the route outside the object's edges and use a short phrase.
Preview the beginning and ending carefully, then download the animation as an MP4 video.
