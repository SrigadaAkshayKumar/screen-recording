This tutorial shows you how to export a Canva logo as a small website favicon.
Start with a square Canva design.
Use a square measuring 512 pixels on each side as your working size.
Copy your logo into the square.
If it includes a long business name, use the symbol or a simple initial instead.
A favicon needs to remain recognisable when displayed very small in a browser tab.
Enlarge the symbol so it fills most of the square but leave a little clear space around the edges.
Centre it on the page, for example use a bold letter inside a coloured circle rather than
shrinking a full business card layout into the icon.
Check the design at a small zoom.
If thin lines disappear or letters become unreadable, simplify the artwork before exporting.
Select Share and Download, then choose PNG.
Enable Transparent Background if your plan supports it and you want an icon without
a solid square behind it.
Otherwise choose an intentional background colour.
Download the file and upload it through your website platform's favicon or site icon setting.
For a Canva website, open the dropdown beside Publish and choose Settings.
In the Site tab, select the favicon, upload the square image, choose Done and publish the update.
Here's the useful distinction.
PNG works for Canva's favicon upload, while another website platform may request different
dimensions or an ICO file.
Follow that platform's requirements.
Finally, open the published website and check the browser tab.
A cached icon may need a refresh before the new version appears.
