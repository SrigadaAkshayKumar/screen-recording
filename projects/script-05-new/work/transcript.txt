This tutorial shows you how to fill empty space around a video with a moving blurred backdrop in Canva.
Prepare two versions of the same footage, the sharp original and a blurred copy.
To create the blurred copy, open the source clip in CapCut's online editor.
Apply a blur video effect across the entire clip and export an MP4 without changing its duration.
Upload both files to Canva.
Add the blurred version first and enlarge it until it covers the whole canvas.
Some of this background copy will be cropped, which is fine because its purpose is to fill the empty areas.
Add the sharp original as a separate video element.
Keep its proportions and resize it until the full picture fits inside the canvas.
Use position to centre it and keep its layer above the blurred version.
Match the timeline start points and the source sections used by both clips.
Keep their speed and duration the same so the backdrop follows the main footage.
Set the background copy's volume to zero to prevent doubled sound.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
This effect blurs an enlarged copy behind the video.
It does not remove or blur the real background around a person inside the sharp foreground clip.
Play the beginning, middle and end to check alignment.
Once the empty space is filled and the sound plays once, download your design as an MP4 video.
