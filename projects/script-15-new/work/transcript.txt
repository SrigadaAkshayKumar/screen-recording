This tutorial shows you how to divide one audio clip into separate sections in a Canva video.
Open your project in the video timeline and select the audio clip.
Move the playhead to the exact point where you want to make the first cut.
Open the selected audio's context menu and choose split audio.
Or, split if that is the label in your timeline layout.
The recording is now divided into two sections at the playhead.
To remove a pause from the middle, move the playhead to the end of that pause and split again.
Select the short section between the two cuts and delete it.
Move the remaining section into the position you want.
For a continuous voiceover, bring it next to the previous section and listen across the join.
If the narration must match a particular scene, place it against that scene instead.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Splitting the audio does not automatically move your visuals to match a new narration length.
Check the timing of captions, clips and graphics after removing a section.
If your sound is still embedded inside an uploaded video, select that video and use audio tools, then extract to create an independently editable audio track first.
Preview both sides of the cut and export the finished design as an MP4 video.
