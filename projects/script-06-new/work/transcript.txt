This tutorial shows you how to remove the background from a video and place the subject over a new design in Canva.
Open a video design and upload your footage through uploads.
Add the clip to the canvas, wait for it to finish processing, and select the video itself.
Click BG Remover in the Editor toolbar.
If your layout places this control inside the editing panel, open Edit and look for the Background Removal tool.
Video Background Removal is included with eligible paid Canva plans.
Wait while Canva processes the clip, then play it and inspect the edges around the subject, especially hair, hands and fast movements.
Choose a new background by adding a colour, image or video behind the cutout, open position and layers.
If you need to move that background below the subject, resize and position the foreground clip to suit your layout.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Canva currently limits this feature to videos whose original length is under 10 minutes.
Trimming a long source inside the design does not change the length of the uploaded file.
Export a shorter source clip first when necessary.
Use footage with a clear subject and good lighting for cleaner edges.
Preview the whole processed section before downloading.
Export as an MP4 video to save the subject combined with your new background.
This gives you a finished video with the background you designed.
