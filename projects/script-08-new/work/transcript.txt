This tutorial shows you how to turn your footage into a short, vertical video for YouTube using Canva.
Start a blank mobile video design, or search Canva's templates for YouTube shorts and choose a portrait layout.
Use a 9x16 canvas so the video fills a vertical phone screen.
Upload your footage and place it in the design.
Trim the opening so viewers reach the useful action quickly.
For this example, build a video of around 30 seconds with one clear point.
Add a short headline using text.
Place it where it is easy to read without covering the subject.
Keep the font large enough for a phone screen and remove any template text that does not belong in your video.
If the clip contains speech, select it and open captions from the toolbar, or find captions through text or apps.
Generate the captions, review the spelling, and choose a readable style.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A caption can be correctly timed and still be hidden by YouTube's interface.
Keep the important text away from the bottom edge and the column of buttons on the right.
Preview the short with sound and then watch it muted to check whether the visuals and captions still explain the point.
Download the design as an MP4 video.
Upload that file to YouTube, then choose the title and other publishing settings there.
