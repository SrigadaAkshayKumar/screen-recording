This tutorial shows you how to add readable subtitles to a talking video in Canva and include them in the downloaded file.
Open a video design, upload your recording and place it on the timeline.
Let the upload and processing finish before you generate the captions.
Open text, find dynamic text and select captions.
Select the talking video as the source and generate the captions.
Play the recording while reading the generated words.
Correct any transcription mistakes before spending time on the final appearance.
Pay particular attention to names and words spoken quickly.
Select a caption to adjust its font, size and colour.
Position the words where viewers can read them without covering the speaker's face or an important demonstration.
Test a section with a bright background and another with a dark background.
If the words become difficult to see, use a stronger contrast or a suitable caption background.
Here's the part.
Many tutorials skip.
Canva's view only accessibility captions are separate from the editable captions you add to a design.
Use the captions editing feature when you want visible subtitles in the finished download.
Watch the video once with sound and once without sound.
Both checks help you catch missing words and captions that are too hard to read.
Choose share, download and mp4 video.
Open the exported video to confirm that the subtitles are part of the picture.
