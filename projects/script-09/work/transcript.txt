This tutorial shows you how to remove an animation from a Canva object or page.
Open your design and select the object whose animation you want to remove.
For example, click the photo rather than the page background.
Click Animate in the toolbar.
Open the relevant element or text tab and choose Remove Animation.
The object stays in place, but its applied animation is cleared.
Play the preview to check the result.
If the object still appears to move, click an empty area of the page.
Open Animate again and inspect the page tab.
Remove the page animation too if you want that page to stay still.
If movement happens only when you change pages, inspect the transition between them.
Open that transition settings and remove it separately.
For an object with a custom motion path, open its animation settings and use Delete Path when needed.
Check any additional motion effects that remain enabled.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Remove Animation clears effects applied by Canva.
It does not freeze movement that is already inside an uploaded video,
animated gif or animated graphic.
Replace that asset with a still image when you want it completely static.
Preview from the beginning so you can check both the entrance and the page change.
The selected animation is now removed and the rest of your design remains ready to edit.
