This tutorial shows you how to generate a short video
from a text prompt using Canva AI.
From Canva's homepage, open Canva AI and choose Video Clip.
Check the usage allowance shown
for your account before generating.
Type a clear prompt in English.
Describe the subject, action, setting, lighting,
and camera movement.
For example, a ceramic coffee cup on a wooden desk
beside a rainy window, soft morning light,
a slow camera push toward the cup.
If you want sound, describe the atmosphere or dialogue
as part of the prompt
and use the sound option available in the tool.
If a reference image option is shown,
you can attach an image to guide the composition.
Submit the prompt and wait for the result.
Preview the whole clip before deciding whether to use it.
Look at hands, faces, object movement,
and any text that appears inside the generated scene.
Choose Use Canva Editor to bring the result
into an editable video project.
Trim the clip, add your own title,
and arrange additional clips to build a longer video.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The current video clip tool generates short clips,
including eight second clips with audio
or six second clips without audio.
It does not turn one prompt into a complete long video.
Plan several short shots and assemble the useful results.
Magic Media's video generator is another tool
inside Canva's apps area
with its own workflow and usage rules.
Use the tool available to your account
rather than expecting every AI option
to behave the same way.
Download the finished edit as MP4 video
and review it before sharing.
