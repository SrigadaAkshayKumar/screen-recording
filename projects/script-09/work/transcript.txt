This tutorial shows you how to draw freehand in Canva, adjust your brush and turn a rough shape into a cleaner one.
Open a whiteboard or another supported Canva design.
From the editor sidebar, select Tools, then Draw.
If your layout shows Draw directly, open it there.
Choose Pen for a clean stroke, Marker for a heavier line or Highlighter to mark something while keeping the content underneath visible.
Select the colour tile and choose a document colour or a custom colour.
Open settings to adjust the stroke thickness and transparency.
Click and drag on the Canvas to draw.
For this example, draw an arrow pointing toward a heading, then write a short note beside it.
Use a thicker stroke for the arrow and a thinner one for the handwriting.
To draw a straight line, hold Shift while dragging.
For a cleaner circle, draw the circle and pause at the end of the stroke for about one second.
Canva's Shape Assist can recognise supported shapes and refine the result.
If you make a mistake, undo the last stroke or choose Eraser and brush over the unwanted section.
Select the cursor or select tool when you finish drawing.
You can now select your drawing like another design element.
Move it, resize it and position it alongside the rest of the page.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Dragging while a pen is active creates another stroke.
Switch back to the Select tool before trying to move an existing drawing.
Choose Share and Download if you need an image or PDF of the finished page.
Keep the Canva design so you can return to your drawing and adjust the layout later.
