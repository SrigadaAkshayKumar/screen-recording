This tutorial shows you how to add matching social media icons in Canva and connect them to your profiles in a clickable design.
Open your design and select Elements.
Search for the platform you need, such as Instagram icon, then open the graphics results.
Choose an icon that matches your design.
Add the other platforms using the same visual style, such as all outline icons or all solid icons.
Avoid mixing a colourful three-dimensional icon with a thin monochrome set.
Resize each icon using a corner handle.
Give the icons a similar visual height, then select the whole row and use position to align them and distribute the spacing evenly.
If the selected graphic has editable colours, use the colour tiles in the toolbar to adjust them.
For a consistent layout, keep the icon backgrounds and surrounding space the same.
Now select the first icon and open Link from the toolbar or its three-dot menu.
Paste the complete address of your social profile and apply the link.
Repeat this for each icon, checking that every platform points to the correct profile.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A social icon is only a picture until you add a hyperlink, and that hyperlink will not survive a PNG or JPG export.
Use an unflattened PDF or a published Canva website when the icons need to be clickable.
For a printed design, add your username or a QR code instead of relying on the icon alone.
Preview the finished design, then open the downloaded PDF or published website and test each icon.
You now have a coordinated row of social icons that leads to the right accounts.
