This tutorial shows you how to place your logo over a video in Canva and keep it visible for the right amount of time.
Open your video project and go to Uploads in the left sidebar.
Click Upload Files, select your logo and wait for it to appear.
A PNG with an existing transparent background works well because it avoids a white rectangle around the logo.
Click the uploaded logo to add it as a separate element.
Drag a corner handle to resize it then move it into a corner of the video.
Leave a little space between the logo and the edge so it stays readable when you post the video.
If the logo disappears behind your footage, select Position, Open Layers and move the logo above the video.
Select the logo and look at its layer in the video timeline.
Adjust the start and end edges so it appears throughout the section you want to brand.
For a design with separate scenes or pages, check each one and copy the logo onto any scene where it is missing.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Placing the logo on the canvas does not guarantee it covers the entire finished video.
Preview every scene change and the last few seconds to check its timing.
When everything looks right, choose Share, Download and MP4 Video.
Your downloaded video now includes the logo.
