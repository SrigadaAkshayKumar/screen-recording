This tutorial shows you how to make a wedding slideshow in Canva with photos, short captions, and background music.
Create a landscape video design for a television or projector.
Search the templates for wedding slideshow and choose a simple layout that keeps the photos prominent.
Upload the pictures you want to use.
Arrange them in a clear order such as childhood, how you met, favorite memories, and the wedding day.
Replace the template photos with your own.
When a photo is inside a frame, double-click it to adjust the crop.
For an important portrait, choose a layout that shows the whole image instead of cutting off faces.
Add a title scene with the couple's names and wedding date.
Keep captions brief and use the same font style throughout the slideshow.
Place the photos in sequence and adjust their durations in the timeline.
Start with around four seconds for a simple photo, then give group pictures and longer captions more time.
Add a subtle transition between scenes and keep the effect consistent.
Use the audio track to add your chosen music, then trim it to the slideshow's length and adjust the volume.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Adding more photos without adjusting the timing can make the slideshow much longer than expected.
20 photos at four seconds each already take 80 seconds before the opening, ending, and transitions.
Play the slideshow all the way through.
Check the photo order, names, captions, and music ending.
Download it as mp4 video.
Test the exported file on the television, projector, or computer that will play it at the event.
You now have a wedding slideshow that runs automatically as a video.
