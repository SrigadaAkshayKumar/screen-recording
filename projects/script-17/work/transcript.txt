This tutorial shows you how to speed up a video clip in Canva so the faster motion is included in the downloaded video.
Create or open a video design.
Go to uploads, add your video and wait for it to finish uploading before editing.
Select the actual video clip, click edit in the toolbar, then find video speed in the side panel.
If speed appears directly in your toolbar, you can open the same controls from there.
Move the speed slider above 1 or enter a value such as 2.
Canva's documented video speed range is 1 quarter speed through double speed.
Preview the clip.
At double speed, 10 seconds of source action plays in approximately 5 seconds.
To speed up only the middle of a clip, first select it in the timeline.
Move the playhead to the start of the section and press S to split.
Split again at the end, select the middle segment and apply the speed change only to that segment.
Check the timeline afterward.
Adjust any remaining scene space and reposition text,
narration or music that should follow the faster action.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Speeding up the picture can make its original sound unsuitable.
Listen to the result and mute the clip's audio if you are using the fast motion under separate narration or music.
Preview the transition into and out of the accelerated section.
Then download an mp4 and check the saved file to confirm that the speed change is part of the export.
