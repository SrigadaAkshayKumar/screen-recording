This tutorial shows you how to remove a caption in Canva by identifying whether it is text,
a generated subtitle, or a playback setting.
First, click the caption you want to remove.
If it behaves like a normal text box, select the text element and press Delete.
Check the timeline selection before deleting so you remove the caption rather than the video underneath it.
If it is a generated caption, open the captions panel.
Find the specific line and use its delete icon.
Preview that moment to confirm the unwanted line has disappeared.
If the caption cannot be selected and only appears while media is playing, open File, then Accessibility.
Turn off Show View Only Captions on Media.
For an individual video, its More menu may also contain the View Only Caption Control.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Turning off View Only Captions changes the playback display.
It does not delete text boxes or generated caption elements already placed in your design.
Check the exact moment again after making the change.
If you still see words, pause and determine whether there is another text element on top of the video.
Captions already embedded in an uploaded video require the original footage without those words.
They are not separate Canva elements.
Once the intended caption is gone, download a new MP4 and check the saved video.
