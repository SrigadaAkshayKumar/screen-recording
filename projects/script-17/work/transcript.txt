This tutorial shows you how to turn a photo into a sticker-style graphic in Canva.
Create a square design and upload a photo with a clear subject.
An object or portrait with visible edges is easier to turn into a clean sticker.
Select the photo and use BG Remover to remove its background.
Check the edges before continuing, especially around hair or small details.
Background removal requires eligible Canva access.
With the cutout selected, open Edit and find Shadows.
Choose Glow to build an outline around the subject.
Set the glow colour to white, reduce blur to zero, and increase its strength so the border looks solid.
Adjust the size until the white edge is visible without swallowing small details.
Place a temporary coloured shape behind the sticker to inspect the border.
This reveals gaps or leftover background patches.
You can add a short caption underneath using a bold font.
Keep the wording brief so it stays readable when the sticker is small.
When you are happy, remove the temporary background shape.
Select Share and Download, choose PNG, and enable Transparent Background if your plan supports that export.
Here's the important detail.
The white outline should remain part of the sticker, while the empty space outside it should be transparent.
A white page alone does not create the same result.
Download the PNG and test it over a coloured background.
For a physical sticker, send the artwork to your printer using their required size and cut line specifications.
A visible white outline is not automatically a machine-readable cutting path.
