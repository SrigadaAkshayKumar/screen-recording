This tutorial shows you how to shorten an audio clip and choose which part of a recording plays in Canva.
Open your video project and select the audio bar in the timeline.
Make sure you have selected the sound itself rather than the video or a text layer.
Hover over the audio's edges to reveal its trim handles.
Drag the left handle inward to shorten the beginning or drag the right handle inward to shorten the end.
Play the result after each adjustment.
For example, if a recording begins with a few seconds of silence, trim that silence away,
then move the shortened clip to the point where the first word should be heard.
If you need a different part of a song while keeping the same playback window, open the audio's menu and choose Adjust.
Move the waveform within the highlighted range until the section you want is selected.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Trimming the edge and moving the whole clip solve different problems.
Trimming changes the selected duration.
Moving changes where that audio plays in your design.
Listen carefully at the new start and end.
Avoid cutting through a spoken word or stopping music at an awkward moment.
Add a short fade if the ending needs smoothing.
Preview the design and download an MP4 video with the trimmed soundtrack.
