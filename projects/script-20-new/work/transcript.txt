This tutorial shows you how to separate a video's soundtrack so you can edit or reuse it independently in Canva.
Open your video design, upload the source clip and wait until it is finished processing.
Add it to the design and select the video directly.
Right-click the video and choose Extract Audio.
You can also open Audio Tools in the toolbar and select Extract.
Canva places the extracted sound on the timeline as a separate audio track.
Select that new track to trim it, reposition it, change its volume or divide it into sections.
You can keep the narration and change the pictures playing above it,
which is useful for adding B-roll or updating a tutorial's visuals.
Preview the sound after extraction.
If you hear doubled audio, check whether the source video is also playing its soundtrack and mute that source clip.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
This feature creates an editable track inside the design.
It does not offer a standalone MP3 or WAV download.
To save an audio-only file, download the finished MP4 and convert that file with a separate audio converter.
The extraction option is available for uploaded videos containing audio.
Canva library footage and embedded videos are not supported by this feature.
Once the track is separated, finish your edits and download an MP4 video to keep the extracted audio with your chosen visuals.
