This tutorial shows you how to create an Instagram quote real with a moving background and readable text in Canva.
Search Canva for an Instagram real template and open a portrait design.
Choose a simple layout with room for the quote or remove the template elements and start with a clean canvas.
Add a background video from your uploads or canvas video library.
A slow moving scene with few distracting details works well.
Trim it to a length that gives viewers time to read the quote comfortably.
Open text, add a text box and type your quote.
Break it into short lines and use a large font.
Add the author's name separately when you are crediting a specific person.
If the words are difficult to read, place a dark or light shape behind them and reduce that shape's opacity.
Select the text and add a simple animation if it helps the words enter cleanly.
Set the text's timeline duration so the quote remains on screen long enough to read.
Add licensed music if you want, keeping it low when the real also includes narration.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
An attractive text animation can delay the moment when the full quote becomes readable.
Count the reading time after the words have appeared.
Preview the real at phone size, export an MP4 video and upload it to Instagram.
