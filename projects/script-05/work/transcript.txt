This tutorial shows you how to make a clear, professional-looking poster in Canva and export it for printing.
From the Canva homepage, search for poster and choose the size you need.
Check the dimensions before starting.
If your printer requests a specific size, create a custom design with those measurements.
Open design and choose a template with a layout that suits your message.
For this example, use an event poster with a headline, one main image and a short block of details.
Replace the headline first, keep it short and make it the largest text on the page.
Add the event date, time and location underneath, followed by one clear action, such as book your place.
Upload your own photo or choose an image from elements.
Use a sharp image with enough space for the text.
If the photo makes the wording difficult to read, place the text over a solid coloured panel.
Change the template's colours to a small, consistent palette.
Use one font for the headline and another readable font for the supporting details.
Align related items so the poster has a clear reading order.
Here's the part many tutorials skip. Zoom out until the whole poster looks small.
If you cannot immediately identify the headline and main image, simplify the layout.
A poster has to communicate before someone stops to read the details.
Proofread the date, address and contact information.
Click Share, select Download and choose PDF Print.
Follow your printer's instructions for bleed and crop marks.
Open the exported file and check its page size and image clarity before sending it to print.
