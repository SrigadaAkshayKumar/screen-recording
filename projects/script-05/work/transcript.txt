This tutorial shows you how to slow down a video clip in Canva and export the result.
Create a video design, open uploads, and import your footage.
Add the clip to the timeline and wait until it has finished uploading.
Select the video itself, then choose Edit from the toolbar.
Find video speed in the editing panel.
If speed appears directly in your toolbar, you can use that shortcut instead.
Set the speed below 1.
A value of 0.5 plays the footage at half speed, while 0.25 plays it at quarter speed.
Start with half speed and preview the movement before slowing it further.
If you only want one moment in slow motion, move the playhead to its start,
right-click the clip, and split it.
Split again at the end of that moment.
Select only the middle section and reduce its speed.
Check the timeline after changing the speed.
The slowed section needs more playback time, so make sure its full action is included
and that any titles or music still line up.
Listen to the clip's audio.
If the slowed sound is distracting, select the video
and lower its volume or mute it before adding separate music.
Here's the part.
Many tutorials skip.
Slowing down ordinary footage cannot recover motion that the camera never recorded.
Start with higher frame rate footage when possible,
and use a smaller slowdown if the result looks jerky.
Select Share, download an MP4 video.
Play the downloaded file to check the movement and audio.
Your slow motion video is now ready.
