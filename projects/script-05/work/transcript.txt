This tutorial shows you how to design a swipeable carousel in Canva and download its slides in the correct order.
From Canva's homepage, search for an Instagram carousel template.
Choose a multi-page design or create a custom design at 1080 x 1350 pixels for a portrait example.
Keep every slide at the same dimensions.
Start with a cover that states one clear benefit, such as five ways to organise your desk.
Use the next three pages for individual tips.
Give each page a short heading, a brief explanation and one useful visual.
Finish with a page that asks the viewer to save the post or try one of the tips.
Replace the template's colours and fonts with your own.
Keep the heading and page number positions consistent and leave enough space around the edges for the text to breathe.
To add another slide, duplicate an existing page and replace its content.
This keeps the design consistent without rebuilding the layout.
Open GridView and drag the pages into their final order.
Read them from beginning to end to make sure each slide follows naturally from the previous one.
Choose Share, Download and PNG.
Select all carousel pages and download them.
If Canva provides a zip file, extract it to access the individual slide images.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The carousel is made from separate images.
A PDF or one long image will not give you the same ready-to-upload slide files for an Instagram image carousel.
When creating the Instagram post, select the images in their intended order and check the upload preview for cropping.
Your cover should appear first and the final action page should appear last.
