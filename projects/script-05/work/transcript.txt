This tutorial shows you how to blend two videos in Canva using a transparent overlay,
then create a smooth transition between consecutive clips.
For an overlay blend, place the first video on the main timeline track.
Add the second video above it and position its timeline segment so both videos play at the same time.
Resize the upper video to cover the area where you want the blend.
Select it, open Transparency from the toolbar and lower the value.
Start near 50%, then adjust until both shots are visible.
Play the overlap and refine the balance.
A bright upper clip may need lower opacity, while a subtle texture can work at a stronger setting.
Select each video's volume control and mute any audio you do not want to mix.
If you want one shot to dissolve into the next instead, arrange both clips consecutively on the main track.
Hover between them, select Add Transition, and choose Dissolve.
Adjust its duration, then preview the cut.
In older page-based designs, the transition control appears between the video pages.
In the updated editor, look between the main track clips.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Lowering transparency keeps both images visible during their overlap.
A dissolve changes from one image to another over time.
Choose the method that matches the result you want.
This opacity method also does not provide the same control
as dedicated screen or multiply blend modes in a compositing editor.
Download a short MP4 test and check the picture and audio together
before exporting the complete video.
