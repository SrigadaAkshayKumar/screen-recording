This tutorial shows you how to add a sound effect to a Canva presentation and control when it plays.
Open your presentation and select the slide where you want the effect.
Open audio, search for a suitable sound, and preview it before adding it.
You can also upload your own recording through uploads.
Show the presentation's timing or timeline controls so you can see the audio in relation to the slides.
Find the sound effect and position it at the moment you want it to play.
For an effect at the beginning of a slide, align its start with that slide's start.
For a timed title reveal, move the effect to the title's appearance time instead.
Trim the effect, if it lasts too long, and reduce its volume so the sound supports your presentation without startling the audience.
Preview the slides in the same playback mode you plan to use.
If you want a fixed sequence, set the slide durations and check the sound against those timings.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A sound placed on a timed presentation is not automatically a sound that triggers whenever you click a particular text box.
Test your presentation mode before using the effect in a live talk.
For a version that preserves your tested timing, download the presentation as an MP4 video.
A PDF download will not preserve the sound effects.
