This tutorial shows you how to edit a faceless YouTube video in Canva, using narration, supporting footage, and text.
Create a landscape video design for a regular YouTube upload.
Open uploads, choose upload files, and import your recorded narration and any footage you plan to use.
Place the narration on an audio track first.
Listen through it and identify where each new point begins.
These moments will guide the visual changes.
Use the plus button on the main timeline track to add footage from uploads or Canva's stock video library.
Match each shot to what the narration actually describes.
Drag the clip edges to adjust their length.
To remove a section, select the clip, position the playhead, and press S to split it.
Make a second split at the end of the unwanted section, then delete that segment.
Add a short heading for each important point.
Use the text element's timeline bar to control when it appears and disappears.
Keep headings short enough to read while the viewer is listening.
If your account offers generated captions, create them and correct the wording and timing before export.
Add background music only after the voiceover is clear, then lower the music so it supports the narration.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Clips with their own sound can compete with your voiceover.
Select each background video and reduce or mute its original audio unless you intentionally want that sound in the edit.
Watch the whole video with headphones.
Check that the visuals follow the narration and that every sentence remains understandable.
Then download the finished video as an MP4.
