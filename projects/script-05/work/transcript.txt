This tutorial shows you how to design a large image in Canva and print it across several sheets, using Acrobat Reader.
In Canva, create a design at the final poster dimensions.
For example, choose a custom size of 60 by 40 cm for a landscape poster.
Upload the image and fit it to the page.
Check the crop carefully so the main subject stays inside the finished poster.
Use a sufficiently detailed original image because enlarging a small picture can make the print blurry.
Choose Share, Download and PDF with the print preset.
Download the single poster page without adding crop marks for this example.
Open the PDF in the desktop version of Adobe Acrobat Reader.
Open Print, choose your printer, and set the paper size to the sheets you actually have, such as A4.
Under Page Sizing and Handling, select Poster.
This divides the large PDF page across several sheets.
Set tile scale to 100% to preserve the poster size created in Canva.
Add a small overlap, such as 5mm, and enable cut marks, if helpful.
Check the preview and sheet count before printing.
A small change in overlap or paper orientation can change how many sheets are required.
Here's the important part.
This method uses Canva for the artwork and Acrobat for the tiled printing.
Choosing multiple in Acrobat does the opposite by fitting several document pages onto one sheet.
Print the tiles, trim the overlapping edges as needed, align the picture, and tape the sheets together from behind.
