This tutorial shows you how to animate a line in Canva and create a simple reveal effect.
Open your design and press L on your keyboard to insert a line.
Drag its endpoints to set the length, then use the toolbar to change the colour and line weight.
Position the line underneath your heading.
Select the line itself and click Animate, then view its element animation options.
Choose an available entrance effect.
Fade introduces the line gently.
If wipe is offered for your selected line, use it and choose a left to right direction
to make the line reveal across the page.
If the line does not offer a suitable reveal animation, replace it with a thin rectangle.
Go to Elements, add a rectangle and make it narrow enough to look like an underline.
Give it the same colour and position, then apply wipe to that shape when available.
Adjust the effect's speed or duration, then preview it.
In a video design, move the line's layer so it appears shortly after the heading.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A straight underline revealing from one side is different from drawing a curved,
handwritten stroke.
A moving line object also does not automatically trace its own shape.
For a handwriting effect, choose an existing animated drawing element
or build a separate animation.
Download the finished design as mp4 video or GIF so the line's motion is included.
