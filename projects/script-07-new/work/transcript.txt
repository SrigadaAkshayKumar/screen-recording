This tutorial shows you how to create a vertical video in Canva with a 9x16 layout.
Click Create a Design and search for Mobile Video.
Open a blank portrait video design, a canvas measuring 1080 pixels wide by 1920 pixels.
Tall is a practical full HD size for this format.
Go to Uploads, upload your video clips and add the first one to the design.
Arrange additional clips in the order you want them to play.
For landscape footage, decide how to frame it.
You can enlarge the clip to fill the vertical canvas and crop the sides,
or keep the full picture visible with space around it.
Double-click the video to adjust its crop and keep the important action in view.
Trim unnecessary footage and add text or graphics.
Keep essential words and faces away from the outer edges,
particularly the bottom and right side where social apps often display controls.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Changing the canvas to portrait does not improve the resolution of your original video.
Enlarging a small clip can make it soft,
so use a clear source and avoid zooming farther than necessary.
Play your design from start to finish.
Check the crop again whenever the subject moves.
Choose Share, Download and MP4 Video.
Select a suitable quality option and save the finished vertical video.
