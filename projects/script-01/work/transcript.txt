This tutorial shows you how to design a custom playmat in Canva and prepare the artwork for printing.
First, get the finished dimensions and artwork requirements from the company printing your mat.
Check the required bleed and safe area, because these vary between suppliers.
On Canva's homepage, choose Custom Size.
Select inches, centimetres or millimetres, enter the required dimensions and create the design.
For example, use 24 by 14 inches only if that matches your chosen product.
Add a background colour or upload your artwork.
Extend the background across the entire print area.
Keep important details away from the edges where trimming or stitching will happen.
Open elements and use simple shapes to create playing zones.
For a card game, use rounded rectangles for card positions and text labels for each area.
For a learning mat, replace those zones with letters, numbers or activity spaces.
Select related objects and group them so you can move each zone together.
Use position to line up repeated shapes and keep the spacing consistent.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Canva's default margin and bleed guides follow Canva's own print specifications.
They may not match an outside playmat supplier.
Use that supplier's template or measurements for the final layout.
Go to share, download and choose the file type your supplier requests.
For a print PDF, choose PDF with the print preset or PDF print if your menu lists it separately.
Add crop marks only when requested.
Check the exported files dimensions and image sharpness before submitting it.
Your custom playmat artwork is now ready for a print proof.
