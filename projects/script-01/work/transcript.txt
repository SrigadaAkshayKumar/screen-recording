This tutorial shows you how to record your own voice directly in Canva and place the recording on a video timeline.
Open your video project and arrange the scenes before recording.
Play through the edit once so you know when each part of your narration should begin.
Open audio in the editor and select record voiceover, allow microphone access when your browser asks,
and check that the correct microphone is selected in the recording controls.
Move to the beginning of the section you want to narrate, start the recording and speak clearly
while following the visuals. Use headphones if you need to hear the video without recording
its sound through your microphone. Stop when you finish and keep the recording. Listen to the result
before continuing. On the timeline, move the audio into position and trim any unwanted silence from
its edges. Here's the part many tutorials skip. If record voiceover is missing from an older project,
create a new blank video design using Canva's new timeline. Open audio there, record the
narration, then copy the audio track into your original design. You can also use record
yourself to capture a talking video, select that recording, and choose audio tools, then extract
audio. Once the separate audio track appears, delete the camera video if you only need your
voice. Preview the narration with your scenes, then download the finished design as an mp4 video.
