Script one, how to make an animated film strip in Canva.
This tutorial shows you how to build a moving film strip
from your own photos in Canva.
Create a landscape video design.
Open elements, add a black rectangle,
and stretch it into a wide horizontal strip
across the middle of the page.
Leave the page background a different color
so the strip stands out.
Open elements again, go to frames,
and add a square or rectangular photo frame.
Resize it so there is black space above and below it,
then duplicate it to create three evenly spaced windows.
Drag a different uploaded photo into each frame.
Double-click each photo to adjust its position
without moving the frame itself.
For the film perforations,
add a small white square above the first photo.
Duplicate it across the top edge,
then copy the row along the bottom.
Keep these squares inside the black strip.
Select the rectangle, photo frames,
and white squares, then click group.
Leave the page background outside the group.
Move the group toward the right side.
With it selected, open animate and choose.
Create an animation.
Drag the entire film strip horizontally toward the left,
then release.
Adjust the movement settings and preview the result.
Here's the useful detail.
Everything must move as one group.
Animating the photo separately
can make them slide away from the black strip.
Adjust the scene length to give the movement enough time
and keep any heading outside the animated group.
Finally, choose share, download, and MP4 video.
Play the downloaded file to check the movement.
