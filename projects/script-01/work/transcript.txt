This tutorial shows you how to turn a still photo into an animated design in Canva.
Open Canva on your computer and create a video design.
Choose a landscape or vertical format, depending on where you will use the finished animation.
Open Uploads, click Upload Files, and choose your photo.
Click the uploaded image to place it on the canvas, then resize and position it.
For this example, keep the photo as a selectable image instead of setting it as the page background.
This lets you animate the photo separately from your text and other objects.
Select the photo and click Animate in the toolbar.
Open the Element Animation options and preview the available effects.
Choose a gentle movement, such as pan, or use fade for a simple entrance.
If the chosen effect includes direction or speed controls, adjust them and play the preview.
Keep the movement small enough that important parts of the image remain visible.
In the video timeline, extend or shorten the image layer to control how long the photo stays on screen.
For example, use 5 seconds for a short product shot.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Downloading as PNG or JPG saves a still image.
To keep the movement, click Share, download and choose MP4 Video.
Use GIF when you need a short animated image without sound.
Download the file and play it to check the framing.
Your still photo is now an animated Canva design.
