This tutorial shows you how to create and export a vertical TikTok video using Canva.
Search for TikTok video from Canva's homepage and choose a template or open a blank portrait video design.
Use a 9x16 layout for a full-screen vertical result.
Upload your clips and arrange them in the order you want.
Trim slow beginnings and unnecessary pauses, then adjust each crop to keep the main subject visible.
Replace the template's sample text with your own opening line.
Keep it short enough to understand while the action is playing.
Add any extra explanation as separate text elements
and match their timeline positions to the relevant moments.
For spoken footage, generate captions and correct names, numbers and any words Canva mishears.
Preview the style over both bright and dark frames.
You can include your own licensed audio in Canva,
or export with the original sound and choose a soundtrack later inside TikTok.
If you include both narration and music, listen for whether the words remain clear.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
TikTok places captions and controls over your video.
Keep essential text away from the bottom area and right edge so viewers can actually see it.
Play the finished edit, then download it as an MP4 video.
Upload the file to TikTok and complete the cover,
caption and publishing settings in the app.
