This tutorial shows you how to design a small printable calendar in Canva for a desk insert, planner, or gift.
Decide the finished size first.
For this example, use a portrait calendar that is 90mm wide and 120mm high.
Create a custom design using those dimensions.
In another Canva tab, search the templates for monthly calendar.
Choose a simple layout with a clear date grid.
Open it, select the calendar elements, copy them, and paste them into your custom size design.
Group the date grid before resizing it with a corner handle.
Position it in the lower part of the page and leave room for a photo or illustration above.
Change the month and year.
Check the weekday headings.
Place the first day under the correct weekday and confirm the final date of that month.
If your template uses separate text boxes for dates, update those boxes individually.
Add a small heading and your chosen photo.
Use a readable font for the numbers and keep decorations away from the date grid.
At this size, clear contrast matters more than detailed artwork.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Changing the month name on a calendar template does not necessarily rearrange the dates.
Verify the entire grid against a reliable calendar for that month and year.
Duplicate the finished page for additional months, then update every date grid and heading.
Download the pages as PDF print.
Print a sample at actual size and measure it.
If you want several mini calendars on one sheet, arrange copies on an A4 or letter design before exporting.
Keep the original small format design for future changes.
