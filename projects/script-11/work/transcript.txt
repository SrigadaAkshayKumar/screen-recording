This tutorial shows you how to build and publish a portfolio website in Canva.
From Canva's homepage, search for portfolio website.
Choose a website template and open it in the editor.
Replace the sample introduction with your name, what you do,
and the type of work you want people to hire you for.
Keep the opening short so visitors can reach your projects quickly.
Upload a small selection of your best work.
For each project, add a clear title, a short description,
and the part you are responsible for.
Show enough detail to explain the result without filling the page with unrelated examples.
Use Add section to place more content on the same page.
If you want separate work, about, or contact pages, use Add page instead.
Add a contact button and use Link to connect it to your contact page or another destination.
Keep your contact details easy to find.
Open Publish website and review settings.
Enable navigation menu when visitors need to move between pages
and turn on Resize on mobile.
Preview the site on desktop and mobile,
checking text size, image placement, and buttons.
Choose a free Canva site address,
customize the available address or sub-path, and publish.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The Design's Editor link is different from the published website address.
Share the live website link so people can browse your portfolio without entering the editor.
Open that address in a private browser window and test the projects and contact link.
Your portfolio website is now ready to share with potential clients or employers.
