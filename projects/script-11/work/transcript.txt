This tutorial shows you how to generate English captions in Canva and choose the correct language settings for your recording.
Open your video project and check that the narration is clear.
If the project contains several recordings, identify the one you want to caption.
Open the captions panel. You can find it under Text, Dynamic Text and Captions, or select your media and use captions on the toolbar.
Next to Automatically detect spoken language, select Edit and choose English.
This tells Canva what language the speaker is using.
Check the translation settings separately. For subtitles that simply follow English speech, leave translation off.
If the recording is spoken in another language and you want English subtitles, select its actual spoken language then choose English under Translate Captions Into.
Select the correct recording in Apply Captions To and generate the captions.
Read through the result while listening. Correct names, contractions and any words that do not match the recording.
For translated captions, also check that the sentence still expresses the intended meaning.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The spoken language setting and the translation language do different jobs.
Choosing English as the spoken language for a Spanish recording does not translate it into English.
Preview the subtitles at the size your audience will watch them, then download the finished design as an MP4 video.
