This tutorial shows you how to remove a text animation in Canva while keeping the wording and formatting.
Open your design and click the animated text box once.
Select the whole box rather than highlighting letters inside it.
Click animate in the toolbar and open the text tab.
Find remove animation and select it.
The words font, colour and position stay in your design.
Preview the page.
If the text still moves with the rest of the layout, click an empty area of the page.
Open animate and inspect the page tab.
A page animation can affect the text even after its individual animation has been removed.
If the text uses a custom motion path, remove that path from its animation settings too.
Clear any additional motion effects you no longer want.
For a video design, check the text layer on the timeline.
Removing its animation does not automatically change the layer's start and end times.
Extend it across the scene if the text should remain visible throughout.
For a live presentation, turn off appear on click if you also want the text visible without waiting for a click.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Text inside an uploaded video, or GIF, is part of that media file.
It cannot be selected as a Canva text box or cleared with remove animation.
Play the page again to confirm the text now appears the way you intended.
