This tutorial shows you how to upload an animated GIF to Canva and keep its movement in the finished design.
First, open the GIF on your computer to confirm that it is animated.
Some files ending in .gif contain only one still frame.
Open your Canva design and select Uploads.
Click Upload Files, choose the GIF, and click Open.
Wait for processing to finish.
Find the uploaded GIF in the panel and click it to add it to the design.
Drag a corner handle to resize it, then move it into position.
Press Play or Open Preview to check the animation rather than judging it from a single thumbnail.
In a video design, inspect the GIF's timing on the timeline and adjust when it appears.
Preview the full section to make sure the movement lasts for the part you want to show.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Uploading an animated GIF does not mean every download format will preserve its animation.
PNG and JPG are still image formats.
Choose GIF for an animated image or MP4 video for a video containing the animation.
If the GIF includes subtle colours or gradients,
MP4 may give a cleaner looking result because GIF uses a limited colour palette.
Select Share, Download and your chosen animated format.
Open the downloaded file and check the movement.
Your GIF is now uploaded and ready to use in Canva.
