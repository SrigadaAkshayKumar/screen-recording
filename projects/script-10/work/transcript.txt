This tutorial shows you how to link your Canva account to Gemini and request a Canva design from your conversation.
Open Gemini in your desktop browser and sign into the Google account you want to use.
Canva's setup guidance uses the personal Google account experience.
Open Settings, then Personal Intelligence, and select Connected Apps.
Scroll to the other section and find Canva.
Turn on Canva's toggle.
When the connection screen opens, sign into the correct Canva account
and follow the prompts to link the accounts.
Review and complete the requested authorization.
Return to Connected Apps and confirm that Canva is enabled.
Then open a new Gemini conversation and select Canva by typing the at symbol
and choosing it from the available apps.
Ask for a specific design, for example, request a birthday invitation in Canva with a blue background,
yellow accents, a large event title, and spaces for the date, time, location, and RSVP details.
Review the returned result and open the provided Canva design.
Replace every sample event detail and adjust the layout in Canva before downloading or publishing it.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The connection depends on the Google account currently active in Gemini.
If Canva is missing, first check which account you are using
and whether connected apps are supported for that account.
If Canva is enabled but stops responding,
return to Connected Apps and switch it off and back on.
If that does not help, use Canva's connection details to unlink it.
Then repeat the sign-in process.
Once linked, Gemini can use the Canva capabilities available through the connection.
