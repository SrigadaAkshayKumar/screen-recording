This tutorial shows you how to turn a few facts into an animated infographic video in Canva.
Create a video design.
Use a landscape layout for a standard YouTube video or a vertical layout for Shorts and Reels.
Plan four short scenes, an opening headline and one scene for each of three facts.
Start with the first scene, add your title and choose two main colours that you will repeat throughout the video.
Add another scene for the first fact.
Use one large number, a short explanation and a relevant icon.
For a comparison, add a chart from Elements and replace its sample data with your actual values.
Duplicate the scene for the other facts, keeping the font sizes and spacing consistent.
Change the numbers, descriptions and icons on each copy.
Select an element and click Animate.
Choose a simple entrance effect, then repeat it consistently across the scenes.
Open the video timeline and drag each element's start and end handles to control when it appears.
Add your voiceover through uploads or place an audio track in the timeline.
Extend each scene until its explanation has enough time to finish.
Keep any background music quiet enough for the narration to remain clear.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Animation speed and element timing are separate controls.
Animate changes how the item moves.
The timeline controls when it appears and disappears.
Use both to reveal the number before its explanation.
Play the video from start to finish and check that each fact can be read comfortably.
Click Share, Download, choose MP4 Video and export the finished sequence.
Your infographic is now a video with a clear sequence of information.
