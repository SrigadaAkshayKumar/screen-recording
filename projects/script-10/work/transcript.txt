This tutorial shows you how to create a diagonal image crop in Canva using a shaped frame.
Open your design and go to Elements.
Open the Frames category, then search for Triangle Frame or Diagonal Frame.
Choose a frame with a visible diagonal edge.
Make sure you are adding an image frame rather than a decorative triangle graphic.
A frame acts as a container for your photo.
Resize the frame on the page.
For a simple split layout, place a large right triangle frame against one side,
leaving the opposite side available for a heading or another image.
Open Uploads and drag your photo directly over the frame.
Wait until the photo fills the frame before releasing.
Double-click the photo to enter its crop controls.
Move the image inside the shape and use the corner handles to zoom
until the subject is positioned correctly.
Click outside to finish.
Rotate the frame if you need the diagonal to slope in the opposite direction,
then check the subject again.
For example, place a portrait inside the triangle
and keep the person's face away from the diagonal edge.
This makes the crop look deliberate
instead of accidentally cutting through an important detail.
Here's the useful detail.
Rotating an ordinary rectangular photo does not create the same diagonal mask.
The frame defines which part of the image remains visible.
The hidden image content stays available,
so you can double-click and reposition it later.
Download the complete design as PNG or JPG
or use a transparent background PNG when your plan supports it.
