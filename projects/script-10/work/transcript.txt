This tutorial shows you how to generate automatic captions in Canva instead of typing every line by hand.
First, decide which recording contains the words you want to caption.
A talking video and a separate voiceover may both be available in the same project.
So check the source before generating anything.
Select your media and open captions from the toolbar.
You can also find the feature through text, dynamic text and captions.
Choose the source in apply captions to, select a style and click generate captions.
Canva creates timed captions from the selected speech.
Play through the result and correct anything that changes the meaning.
Check that a caption appears when its words are spoken, rather than during an unrelated scene.
If a line is difficult to understand, listen to that moment again before rewriting it.
Keep punctuation natural and avoid turning one short spoken phrase into a crowded block of text.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Automatic captions are a first transcription, not a final proofreading pass.
A convincing looking caption can still contain the wrong name, number or negative word.
Review the complete video, including its first and last lines.
Make sure the captions remain readable when the video is viewed at a smaller size.
Download the design as an MP4 video and check the saved result before uploading it to your channel.
