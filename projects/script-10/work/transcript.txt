This tutorial shows you how to create mug artwork in Canva and prepare the correct file for printing.
First, decide who will print the mug.
For Canva print, search for mug on the homepage and choose a mug template.
For another supplier, get that supplier's exact artwork dimensions
and start a custom-sized design using those measurements.
Choose a background colour or upload an image.
Add a name, quote or logo, then adjust the font and spacing until the design is readable at the actual mug size.
If the artwork wraps around the mug, place important details away from the handle area and the outside edges.
Use the supplier's safe area guide to check both sides.
Duplicate the page to compare alternate colours or versions.
Keep the flat print artwork separate from any product preview.
To create a preview, open apps and search for mockups.
Choose a mug mockup and place your design onto it.
This lets you check the overall placement before printing.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A photo of your design on a mug is a mockup, not the artwork file the printer needs.
Export the original flat design for production and keep the mockup for showing the product.
For Canva print, open the mug printing options and inspect the proof before ordering.
For another supplier, download the requested PNG or print PDF and confirm its dimensions.
If you use a transfer or sublimation process, follow your printer's mirroring instructions
so the artwork is mirrored exactly once.
Your mug design is now ready for a print test.
