This tutorial shows you how to upload an SVG graphic to Canva and use it in a design.
Open your design, select Uploads and click Upload Files.
Choose the file ending in .svg, then click Open.
Wait for the graphic's thumbnail to appear and click it to place the SVG on the page.
Drag a corner handle to resize the graphic.
SVG artwork is useful for logos and icons because its vector shapes can scale cleanly
without the same pixelation as a small bitmap image.
With the graphic selected, check the toolbar for color swatches.
If Canva exposes its colors, click a swatch to change that color.
Not every SVG provides the same editing options after importing.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Canva does not give you a full vector path editor for an uploaded SVG.
You may be able to change colors, but editing individual anchor points or rewriting outlined text
requires the original vector editing software.
If the SVG fails to upload, simplify it in a vector editor.
Convert live text to outlines, remove unnecessary hidden objects and save a compatible SVG copy.
Canva currently lists a size limit of under 3 megabytes for SVG uploads.
Upload the simplified copy and check its appearance before building the rest of your design around it.
Your SVG is now ready to position, resize and reuse in Canva.
