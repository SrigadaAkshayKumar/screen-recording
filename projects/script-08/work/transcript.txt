This tutorial shows you how to add clickable text and buttons to a PDF exported from Canva.
Open your document and select the words you want people to click.
If the link should cover a whole button, select the button shape instead.
Choose Link from the floating toolbar.
Paste the full destination address into the link field and confirm with Done.
If link is hidden, check the toolbar's More Options.
Repeat this for each destination, such as your website, booking page or contact page.
Use labels that explain what will open, rather than repeating click here throughout the document.
For a button made from a shape and a separate text box, add the same destination to both.
This makes the label and the surrounding button area useful click targets.
When you are finished, choose Share and Download.
Select PDF with the digital preset, or PDF Standard.
If your menu uses the separate file type names.
Open Advanced Settings if needed and leave Flatten PDF turned off.
Download the file.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Downloading them a design as a PNG or JPG removes the clickable link behaviour.
A flattened PDF also does not preserve Canva's hyperlinks.
Use a regular PDF export for a document people need to click through.
Open the downloaded PDF in a browser or PDF reader and test every link.
Check that each destination is correct and that any linked private documents have the right access settings.
Your Canva PDF now includes working clickable links.
