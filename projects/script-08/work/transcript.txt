This tutorial shows you how to place a design onto a realistic product mock-up in Canva.
First, prepare the artwork you want to display.
If it is a separate Canva design, download that artwork as a PNG and upload it into your mock-up project.
Open a new design for the finished product image in the Editor sidebar, select Elements.
Click the search bar and choose the mock-ups category.
You can also find Canva's mock-ups tool through apps.
Choose the type of product you need such as a framed print, a t-shirt, a mug or a device screen.
Add the mock-up to the page.
Open uploads and drag your artwork onto the mock-up's image area.
Wait for Canva to fit the design to the surface.
Select the mock-up and open its available editing controls.
Reposition the artwork and adjust the crop or fit so the important parts remain visible.
Apply the changes when you are happy with the placement.
Resize the finished mock-up on your page, then add a background and any product description you need.
For an online listing, make the product large enough to inspect without crowding the image with text.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The artwork and mock-up surface need compatible proportions.
A wide design placed inside a tall frame can lose its edges when it fills the frame.
Adjust the crop or prepare a version that matches the surface.
Zoom in to inspect small lettering and the product edges.
Then choose Share, Download and PNG or JPG to export your finished product image.
The mock-up is now ready to show how your design could look on the selected product.
