This tutorial shows you how to put a written caption on an animated GIF in Canva and keep the result animated.
Create a video design with the dimensions you want.
Open Uploads, select Upload Files, and choose your GIF.
Wait for the upload to finish, then place the GIF in the design and resize it to fit.
Open Text and select Add a Text Box.
Type your caption and move it above, below, or over the animation.
Choose a clear font and a colour that stays visible as the GIF changes.
For a caption that should stay visible throughout the GIF, extend the text element to cover the complete animation on the timeline.
For a caption that appears later, move the text to that moment and shorten its duration.
Preview one complete loop.
Check that the text does not cover the important action and does not disappear before viewers can read it.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A GIF has no audio track for Canva to transcribe.
Its captions are text overlays you write yourself, rather than automatic subtitles generated from speech.
Select Share, Download, and choose GIF as the file type.
Choose the page you want and download it.
Open the exported GIF in a browser to check the animation and caption together.
Choosing PNG or JPG instead would create a still image.
