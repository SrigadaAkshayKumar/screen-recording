This tutorial shows you how to design a wedding invitation card in Canva for printing or digital sharing.
Search Canva for wedding invitation and choose a template with the correct card dimensions.
If you have already chosen envelopes, match the invitation size to the envelopes before editing.
Open a template and replace the couple's names first.
Keep them prominent, then add the wedding date, ceremony time, venue name and full address.
Add a short RSVP line with the reply deadline and contact details.
Put extra information, such as travel directions or a detailed schedule, on a separate page so the invitation stays easy to read.
Change the colours to match the wedding theme.
Use a decorative font for the names and a simpler font for the important details.
Adjust the line spacing so the text feels balanced without becoming crowded.
If you want to include a photo, upload it and drag it into a frame.
Double-click the frame photo to reposition the crop, keeping faces away from the edge.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Send the proof to someone else and ask them to read the date, time and location allowed.
Familiarity makes it easy to overlook a wrong digit or missing venue detail.
For printed invitations, download PDF print and use the bleed settings requested by your printer.
Print a sample at actual size to check small text.
For digital sharing, choose PNG or an unflattened PDF.
If your RSVP uses a link, use the PDF or share the Canva Design link because a downloaded image does not preserve clickable buttons.
Your invitation is now ready to share in the format your guests will use.
