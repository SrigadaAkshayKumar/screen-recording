Script 8, How to make a foldable business card in Canva.
This tutorial shows you how to design a business card
that folds in half using a custom Canva layout.
First, decide the finished folded size
and folding direction.
For this example, the closed card will be 85 millimeters
wide and 55 millimeters tall with a horizontal fold.
Create a custom design measuring 85 by 110 millimeters.
This is the unfolded sheet
before adding any printer required bleed.
Open file, then rulers and guides, and show the rulers.
Add a horizontal guide halfway down the page at 55 millimeters.
The guide marks the fold.
Use page one for the outside spread.
In a top fold layout,
place the front cover on the lower half.
The upper half becomes the back cover
and usually needs its artwork rotated 180 degrees
relative to the front.
Add page two for the inside spread.
Place the inside information in the two panels
and keep text away from the fold and outer edges.
Here's the essential check.
Make a paper mock-up before ordering.
Mark each panel, print both spreads, fold the sheet,
and confirm which way the covers and inside text face.
Duplex settings and printer templates
can change the required orientation.
So use your print provider's panel layout
for the final file.
Remove any actual line you drew to represent the fold.
A ruler guide will not print, but a decorative line will.
Download a PDF with the print preset
and the bleed settings requested by your printer.
Proof a folded sample before producing the full batch.
