Script four, how to blur a face in a Canva video.
This tutorial shows you how to blur moving faces
in footage used in Canva
with the BG Beeler Face Blur app.
On screen, a Canva video project
and the apps search panel.
Open your design, select apps in the left sidebar
and search for BG Blur or Face Blur.
Open the Face Blur integration from BG Blur.
This is a third party tool,
so check the credit requirements and file limits
shown in its panel before processing.
Connect your BG Blur account if prompted.
Upload the original video through the apps upload control,
then choose the blur strength.
If the app offers different styles,
select the one you want to use.
Run the face blurring action
and wait for the processed result.
Add that version to your Canva design,
replacing the unblurred footage
in the section you are editing.
Play the entire clip and check that every face
you intend to hide remains covered.
Pay special attention when someone turns sideways,
moves quickly or enters the frame.
Increase the blur strength or process the clip again
if details remain recognisable.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A blur shaped graphic placed over one frame
does not automatically follow a moving face.
You need a processed video with tracking
or you must adjust the cover throughout the movement.
If the integration is unavailable
or your clip exceeds its limits,
process it on the BG Blur website
and upload the resulting video to Canva.
Finish your text and layout,
then export the completed design as an MP4 video.
