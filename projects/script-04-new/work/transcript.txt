This tutorial shows you how to blur moving faces in footage used in Canva with the BG Beeler Face Blur app.
Open your design, select apps in the left sidebar, and search for BG Blur or Face Blur.
Open the Face Blur integration from BG Blur.
This is a third-party tool, so check the credit requirements and file limits shown in its panel before processing.
Connect your BG Blur account if prompted.
Upload the original video through the app's upload control, then choose the blur strength.
If the app offers different styles, select the one you want to use.
Run the Face Blurring action and wait for the processed result.
Add that version to your Canva design, replacing the unblurred footage in the section you are editing.
Play the entire clip and check that every face you intend to hide remains covered.
Pay special attention when someone turns sideways, moves quickly, or enters the frame.
Increase the blur strength or process the clip again if details remain recognisable.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A blur-shaped graphic placed over one frame does not automatically follow a moving face.
You need a processed video with tracking, or you must adjust the cover throughout the movement.
If the integration is unavailable or your clip exceeds its limits, process it on the BG Blur website and upload the resulting video to Canva.
Finish your text and layout, then export the completed design as an MP4 video.
