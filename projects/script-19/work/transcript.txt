This tutorial shows you how to turn an illustration into a whiteboard style drawing video using the Speedpaint app inside Canva.
Start a landscape video design and set the page background to white.
Prepare one clear illustration for the first scene.
Simple outlines and a small number of objects make the drawing easier to follow.
If you build the scene from Canva elements, arrange the illustration on its own page and download that page as a PNG.
This gives the app one image to animate.
Open apps in the left sidebar and search for Speedpaint.
Select the app and complete any setup it requires.
Upload your illustration into the app.
Review the available drawing and duration settings, then generate a preview using the options shown in your version of Speedpaint.
Check any credit requirement before starting the generation.
Add the generated drawing video to your Canva design.
Fit it to the scene and play it once to make sure the full illustration is revealed before the clip ends.
For a longer explanation, create another simple illustration.
Generate its drawing video and place it after the first scene.
Add your narration and adjust the scene lengths to match the explanation.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Canva's whiteboard design type is a collaboration canvas.
Choosing it does not automatically turn your drawings into a recorded hand drawing animation.
In this workflow, Speedpaint generates the animated video from your image.
Keep important captions as separate Canva text boxes if you want precise control over when they appear.
Position them in the video timeline after the relevant drawing is revealed.
Preview the entire sequence.
Check the narration and timing, then download the finished design as MP4 Video.
