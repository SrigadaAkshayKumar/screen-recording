This tutorial shows you how to correct Canva's caption language settings when the subtitles use the wrong language or misread the speech.
Open the captions panel and inspect the selected recording.
If your design contains different speakers or languages, make sure you are working with the correct source.
Remove the incorrect generated captions before creating a replacement set.
Then select Edit beside Automatically detect spoken language and choose the language actually spoken in the recording.
Check Translate captions into as well.
Leave translation off when you want subtitles in the original language or choose the language you want viewers to read.
Use Apply captions 2 to include the correct recording.
Generate the captions again and compare the opening sentence with the audio.
If the language is now correct but some words are still wrong, listen for unclear speech, background noise or music covering the voice.
Correct remaining transcription mistakes manually.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Recognition and translation are separate settings.
The spoken language describes the audio, while the translation language describes the subtitles you want to create.
For example, Spanish speech with English subtitles needs Spanish as the spoken language and English as the translation language.
Review names and mix language phrases carefully before exporting.
Download a new MP4 and check that the replacement captions appear in the intended language.
