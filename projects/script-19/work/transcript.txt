This tutorial shows you how to bring a Word manuscript into Canva and use its text to
build a designed PDF e-book.
First, save your manuscript as a Word .docx file.
Use clear chapter headings and remove comments or tracked change material you do not want
carried into the working copy.
Open Canva's homepage and drag the Word file onto it.
You can also use upload files.
Wait for the import, then open the imported document from projects.
Check that the chapter order, paragraphs, images and special characters came through correctly.
Review any changed fonts or formatting before starting the e-book layout.
Keep the imported manuscript open in one tab.
In another tab, search Canva for an e-book template and choose the page size and style you want.
Replace the cover title, author name and image.
Then copy the first chapter heading and a manageable section of body text from the imported manuscript
into the template's text boxes.
Use consistent font sizes, line spacing and margins.
Duplicate a body page to continue the chapter, replacing the text as you go.
Add chapter opening pages, images and page numbers where needed.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Importing Word does not automatically typeset the entire manuscript into an e-book template.
In a normal Canva page design, long paragraphs do not automatically flow through every following page.
Divide the text deliberately and check for missing or duplicated paragraphs.
When the book is finished, download PDF standard for a digital reading copy
or use the printer's required PDF settings for a printed version.
Open the exported PDF and inspect every page.
This workflow produces a fixed layout PDF e-book,
so check readability at the size your readers will actually use.
