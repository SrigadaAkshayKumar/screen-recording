This tutorial shows you how to upload a PNG image to Canva, including a PNG with a transparent background.
Open the design, select uploads, and click upload files.
Choose the file ending in .PNG and click open.
Wait for the thumbnail to load, then click it to place the image on your page.
Drag a corner handle to resize it and move it to the correct position.
For a transparent logo or cutout, use a coloured page background to check its edges.
The page colour should show through the transparent areas around the subject.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
.PNG supports transparency, but a .PNG file can also contain a solid white background.
Uploading that file does not automatically remove the white area.
If you see a white rectangle, check the original .PNG.
Use a version that already has transparency or remove the background with an appropriate editing tool before uploading the corrected file.
Using an already transparent .PNG does not require a paid background removal feature.
Downloading a new Canva design with a transparent background is a separate feature and may depend on your plan.
Also avoid enlarging a small .PNG too much.
It is a raster image, so its sharpness depends on the original pixel dimensions.
Your .PNG is now uploaded and ready to use in the design.
