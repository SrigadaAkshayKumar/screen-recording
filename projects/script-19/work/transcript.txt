This tutorial shows you how to create a client invoice from a Canva template.
From the Canva homepage, search for invoice and choose a template with enough room for your services or products.
Replace the sample business name, logo, address and contact details with your own.
Add the customer's billing information in the client section.
Enter a unique invoice number, the issue date and the payment due date.
Keep the date format consistent throughout the document.
Now replace the sample line items.
For each item, enter a clear description, quantity, unit price and line total.
For a simple example, three editing sessions at 50 euros each produce a line total of 150 euros.
Check that the subtotal matches all line items.
Add any applicable adjustments and the final amount due.
Include the currency so the customer does not have to guess which currency the amounts use.
In the payment section, enter the agreed payment method and the reference the customer should include.
Here's the important detail.
A standard Canva invoice design is a layout, so do not assume the totals recalculate when you change a quantity or price.
Verify the arithmetic separately.
Remove unused rows and every piece of placeholder information.
Check that the invoice contains the business and tax details required for your situation.
Choose Share, Download and PDF with the digital preset for an emailed copy.
Open the PDF and proofread it before sending.
Keep an editable master template, then duplicate it for the next invoice instead of overwriting the previous customer's record.
