This tutorial shows you how to edit a vertical reel in Canva using your own footage.
From Canva's homepage, create an Instagram reel or another vertical video design.
Use a 9x16 canvas, such as 1080x1920 pixels.
Open uploads, click upload files and select your footage.
Drag the clips onto the main timeline track in the order you want them to appear.
Trim each clip by dragging its edges.
Remove long pauses and keep the opening focused on the action or message that introduces the reel.
If your footage is horizontal, select the video on the canvas and adjust its crop and position to fit the vertical frame.
Keep the person or main subject visible throughout the clip.
Add a short opening heading from the text panel.
Set its timing on an upper timeline layer so it appears over the correct moment.
Add other text boxes only where they help explain the video.
Keep important wording away from the extreme edges,
especially the bottom and right side where Instagram's interface can cover the picture.
Add music or your uploaded voiceover on an audio track.
Adjust the music volume so any speech stays easy to hear.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A vertical canvas does not guarantee that every imported clip is framed correctly.
Review each clip separately, especially when your subject moves across a horizontal recording.
Play the full reel, check every crop and text layer, then download it as MP4 Video.
Review the file on your phone before uploading it to Instagram.
