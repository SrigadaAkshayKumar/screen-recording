This tutorial shows you how to combine an outline and a shadow in Canva using two aligned text layers.
Open your design, add a text box and type the heading.
Choose the final font, size, spacing and alignment before making a copy.
Duplicate the text using CTRL and D on Windows or CMD and D on a Mac.
Select the front copy, open Effects and choose Outline.
Pick a contrasting outline colour and adjust its thickness so the words remain easy to read.
Select the second copy using Position and Layers.
Give that copy a shadow effect labelled Drop or Shadow depending on your editor, then adjust its direction, distance and softness.
Move the shadow copy behind the outlined copy using the layer controls.
Select both text boxes, open Position and align their horizontal centres and vertical middles.
The letters should overlap exactly, with only the shadow extending beyond the front text.
If a duplicate was offset when created, align it precisely instead of estimating its position.
Here's the practical advantage, the two effects stay on separate copies, so choosing one effect cannot replace the other layer's effect.
Group the copies when you are satisfied, move and resize the group to keep the layers together.
To change the wording later, edit both copies and keep their fonts identical.
Otherwise the letters and shadow can stop lining up.
Preview the heading against its actual background and download a test image.
If the shadow is hard to see, increase its offset slightly or choose a stronger contrasting colour.
