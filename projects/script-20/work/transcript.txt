This tutorial shows you how to make a countdown ranking video in Canva, revealing fifth place first and first place last.
Create a portrait video design for a short or real, or choose landscape for a regular YouTube upload.
Add a blank scene to the main track and extend it to about 20 seconds.
Add a title describing the ranking.
Under it, build five rows with a rank number, an image frame and a short name.
Put number one at the top and number five at the bottom.
Fill the frames with your images and enter the names.
Keep the photos, labels and spacing consistent so each entry is easy to compare.
Select the image and name for fifth place and group them.
Repeat for the other entries.
Leave the rank numbers visible throughout so viewers can see the empty positions before each reveal.
Use each groups bar in the timeline to control its start time.
Show fifth place at the beginning, fourth at four seconds, third at eight seconds,
second at 12 seconds and first at 16 seconds.
Let every revealed entry remain visible until the end.
Apply a simple entrance animation to each group.
Add a small sound effect at each reveal or upload narration introducing the entries.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Viewers need time to see the completed ranking.
Keep the full board visible for a few seconds after number one appears,
instead of ending immediately on the final reveal.
If the ranking uses a score or a specific criterion, show that clearly in the heading or labels.
Preview the countdown, check that the names match the numbers and download the finished video as an MP4.
