This tutorial shows you how to animate text and graphics in Canva and export the result as a video.
Click the heading you want to animate, then select animate from the toolbar.
Open the text animation options and choose a style such as a fade or rise that is available in your account.
Hover over an option to preview it, then click to apply it, select the photo or graphic,
open animate again and choose an animation for that element.
To animate the overall page, use the page tab in the animation panel instead.
For custom movement, select an element, open animate and choose create an animation.
Drag the element along the path you want it to follow, adjust the available movement settings, then click done.
In a video design, use the element's timeline handles to choose when it appears and disappears.
Preview the design to check that each animation has time to play.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Element timing and animation speed are different controls.
Timeline handles control when the element is visible.
The animate panel controls the motion settings available for its animation.
To remove an effect, select the element, return to animate and choose remove animation.
When the result looks right, choose share, download and mp4 video.
Use GIF if you need an animated image instead.
A PNG or JPG download will show a still image.
Your Canva design now includes animation you can preview and export.
