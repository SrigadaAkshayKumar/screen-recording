This tutorial shows you how to flip a video horizontally in Canva and export the mirrored result.
Create a video design or open your existing project.
Go to Uploads, import your video, and add it to the timeline.
Select the actual video on the canvas.
In the toolbar, choose Flip, then Flip Horizontal.
The left and right sides of the footage switch places immediately.
If the video is attached as the page background and you cannot access Flip,
right-click it and detach it from the background first.
Flip the video, then resize and position it to fill the canvas again.
Choose Flip Vertical only when you want to swap the top and bottom of the picture.
For an ordinary mirror effect, keep using Flip Horizontal.
Play the entire clip and check the framing.
If the project contains several separate clips, select and mirror each clip that needs the effect.
Flipping one selected clip does not automatically change the others.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Mirroring the footage also reverses any writing, signs or logos already recorded inside it.
Check those details before using the effect.
Text you add separately in Canva can stay readable because you are flipping the video itself.
If a clip was already mirrored, applying Flip Horizontal again returns it to the opposite orientation.
Compare the result with something recognizable in the original footage.
When the image looks correct, select Share, Download, and MP4 Video.
Open the downloaded file to confirm the mirror effect appears in the final video.
