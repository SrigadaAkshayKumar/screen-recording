This tutorial shows you how to create a printable sticker sheet in Canva for full sheet adhesive
paper that you cut by hand.
Create an A4 or US letter design to match your sticker paper.
Use a white page background and leave a clear margin around the outside for your printer's
non-printable area.
Add your first sticker using text, shapes, illustrations or an uploaded image.
For a simple label, place a short phrase inside a coloured circle or rounded rectangle.
Select the sticker's parts and group them.
Resize the group to the intended sticker size, then duplicate it to create more stickers with
matching dimensions.
Change the wording, colours or illustrations on the copies.
Keep enough white space between stickers so you can cut around each one comfortably.
Use position to align the rows and distribute the spacing.
Check that none of the stickers crosses the page margin or overlaps another design.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A general sticker sheet will not automatically align with pre-cut label paper.
For pre-cut sheets, you need a layout that matches that exact product's label size,
spacing and margins.
This example uses uncut adhesive paper instead.
Click Share, download and choose PDF print.
For a home printed sheet, leave crop marks and bleed off unless your specific workflow
needs them.
Print a test on ordinary paper with actual size or 100% scaling.
Measure the stickers and check the outer margins.
Avoid feet to page if you need the dimensions to stay unchanged.
Once the test is correct, print on adhesive paper compatible with your printer.
Let the ink dry, then cut the stickers by hand.
Your printable sticker sheet is now ready.
