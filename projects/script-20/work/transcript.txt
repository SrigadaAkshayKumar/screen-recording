This tutorial shows you how to arrange clips, layers and audio using Canva's video timeline.
Open a video design. The timeline appears at the bottom of the editor.
If you are viewing page thumbnails, use the page's pencil icon when available to enter the video timeline.
The vertical line is the playhead.
Drag it or click the time ruler to inspect a particular moment.
Press the play button to watch the sequence.
Your main track holds the primary clips, which play one after another.
Drag clips along that track to change their order.
Upper tracks hold overlays, including headings, graphics and additional video.
Items on higher tracks appear over lower items when they overlap in time.
Audio has its own track area below the visuals.
Select a clip or element and drag its edges to adjust its start and end.
Drag a whole overlay left or right to move its timing without changing its length.
To split an item, select it, place the playhead at the cut and choose More, then Split.
You can delete a segment or edit the resulting pieces separately.
Use timeline zoom to make short clips easier to inspect.
This enlarges the timeline view. It does not change the video's resolution or playback speed.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Timeline placement controls when an object appears,
while its canvas position controls where viewers see it.
Moving a text layer later does not move the text to a different part of the screen.
Preview the video, check how the layers overlap,
and export as MP4 video when the sequence is ready.
