This tutorial shows you how to find music you are allowed to use and add it to a Canva video.
Open your design and select Audio from the sidebar.
If it is hidden, open Elements and browse the Audio category.
Or find Audio through Apps.
Search for a mood or genre such as calm, cinematic, or upbeat.
Listen to a preview, then open the track's three-dot information menu before choosing it.
Check the usage information.
For a monetized video or business project, look for stock music marked commercial use allowed.
Popular music has a separate license and is restricted to personal, non-commercial use.
A free track is still copyrighted. Permission comes from its license.
Place the playhead where the music should begin, then click the track to add it.
Adjust its position and length on the timeline.
Lower its volume when your video also includes speech.
You can also upload music you obtained elsewhere through uploads.
Check that its license covers your intended use and follow any attribution requirement.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Licenced music can still trigger an automated content ID claim.
Follow Canva's account verification guidance before downloading for YouTube
and retain the track and license information in case you need it.
Preview the soundtrack, download an MP4 video,
and check that the music starts and ends where you intended.
