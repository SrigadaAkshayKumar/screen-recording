This tutorial shows you how to design an easy-to-read menu in Canva for a cafe, restaurant or takeaway.
Search Canva for menu and choose a template in your intended paper size.
Start with a layout that has enough room for your actual number of items.
Replace the restaurant name and add your logo through uploads.
Set your brand colours before editing the rest of the menu so the design stays consistent.
Organise the items into categories such as breakfast, lunch and drinks.
Make the category headings larger than the item names and leave a clear gap between sections.
For each item, add the name, a short description and its price.
Keep descriptions brief.
Use separate text boxes for the prices and align them to the same right-hand position.
Build one complete item row, group its elements and duplicate it.
This keeps the text size, spacing and price alignment consistent as you fill out the menu.
Use food photos only where they help customers understand the offering.
One good image can work better than several small pictures that force the menu text to shrink.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Finish entering all the items before perfecting the spacing.
A layout that looks balanced with sample text
may overflow when you replace it with real dish names and descriptions.
Check every price, remove leftover template text
and confirm that any dietary symbols match the information you intend to provide.
Download PDF print for physical menus or PDF standard for a digital copy.
Print a sample at actual size and read it at a normal table distance.
Adjust small text before producing the final copies.
