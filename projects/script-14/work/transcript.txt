This tutorial shows you how to embed an online video in a Canva design and explains when
you should upload a video file instead.
Copy the video's normal share link from its source, return to your Canva design, open
apps and search for embeds, or the supported video source app.
Open the app, paste the link and choose Add to Design.
Canva places the video player on the page, resize it with the corner handles and position
it beside your text or other content.
Keep its original proportions so the player does not look stretched.
Preview or present the design and test playback.
Make sure the source video is available to the people who will watch it.
A private or restricted video may work for you while remaining inaccessible to your audience.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
An embed points to a video hosted elsewhere.
It does not turn that online video into a local clip inside Canva's video renderer.
If you need the footage to appear in a downloaded MP4, use a video file you own or have permission
to use.
Open uploads, choose upload files, wait for processing to finish and place that uploaded
clip in the design.
You can then trim it, adjust its volume and include it in a normal MP4 export.
Also test presentation playback using the internet connection you expect to use.
An online embed may need network access and can still require the viewer to press play.
Use an embed for supported online playback and an uploaded clip when the finished video
needs to contain the footage itself.
