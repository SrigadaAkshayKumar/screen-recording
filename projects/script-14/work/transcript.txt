This tutorial shows you how to copy an editable Canva design from one account into another account.
Sign into the account that currently has the design.
Open the correct team or workspace, go to Projects, and find the design you want to copy.
Open Share, enter the email address of your other Canva account, and set the permission to Can Edit.
Share the design with that account. Now switch to the destination account.
Using a separate browser profile can make it easier to keep the two accounts apart.
Open the shared design from the invitation or Canva's notifications.
Confirm that the profile and workspace shown in Canva are the ones where you want the copy stored.
Open File and choose Make a Copy.
Rename the copy, then find it in the destination account's projects.
Open the new copy and check the pages, images, fonts, and audio.
Premium features and content still depend on the destination account's access.
Copying a design does not transfer a paid subscription or a brand kit.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Sharing the original is not the same as creating an independent copy.
Until you use Make a Copy, both accounts are opening the same shared design.
Keep the original until you have verified that the destination copy works.
Repeat this process for each design when moving between separate accounts.
Canva's folder copy workflow is intended for copying between teams within the same account.
If external sharing is blocked, the source team's administrator may need to allow it before you can complete the copy.
