This tutorial shows you how to add animation to a caption in Canva while keeping the text easy to follow.
Open your video and select the caption you want to animate.
Click Animate on the toolbar to open the available effects.
Look for the captions section.
Depending on the features available in your account, you may see Reveal, Highlight or Snake.
Choose an effect and preview the spoken phrase from its beginning.
You can also try a regular text animation when it suits your design.
Keep the movement simple so the animation does not distract from the instruction or story.
If the selected effect offers speed or duration controls, adjust them and preview again.
Make sure the viewer can read the complete phrase before the caption disappears.
Repeat the process for other captions you want to customize.
For a consistent animated style across the video, use a caption style pack through the captions panel.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Animation controls how the caption moves, while the captions timeline segment controls when it appears and disappears.
If the words are out of sync with the voice, adjust the segment timing rather than trying to fix it with animation speed.
Play the section once with sound and once without it.
Download the finished design as an MP4 video to preserve the moving captions.
