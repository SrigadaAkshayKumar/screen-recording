This tutorial shows you how to make a T-shirt graphic in Canva and prepare it for printing.
From Canva's homepage, search for T-shirt, choose a template for Canva print,
or create a custom-sized design using the artwork dimensions supplied by your own printing company.
Start with one main idea – a short phrase, a simple illustration, or a strong combination of the two.
Add your text and choose a font that stays readable from a distance.
Upload your artwork or add suitable design elements.
Arrange everything within the printable area and use position to centre the composition.
Check thin lines and small text, at the size they will actually appear on the shirt.
Test the design against a colour similar to the shirt you plan to use.
Light artwork may need a dark shirt, while dark artwork needs enough contrast on a lighter shirt.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The shirt preview and the print graphic are different files.
A picture of a T-shirt is useful for showing the product,
but the printer usually needs your original flat artwork.
For a printer requesting a transparent PNG, remove any full-page background shape.
Select Share and download, choose PNG,
and enable transparent background if your plan supports it.
Check the final pixel dimensions against the printer's requirements.
If you are ordering through Canva Print, use its T-shirt printing options
and review the placement proof instead.
Follow your printer's instructions for file type,
colour and any mirroring.
Check a sample before producing a full batch.
Your T-shirt artwork is now ready for printing.
