This tutorial shows you how to create a freeze frame moment in a Canva video
by inserting a still image between two moving sections.
First, open your source video in VLC Media Player and pause on the moment you want to hold.
Open the video menu and choose Take Snapshot to save that frame as an image.
Using a video snapshot avoids capturing the player buttons or surrounding desktop.
Return to Canva and add the same video to a video design.
Move the playhead to the matching moment, select the clip and choose More, then Split.
You now have a section before the freeze and a section that resumes afterward.
Upload the saved snapshot to Canva and insert it on the main timeline between those two sections.
Set the image duration to around two seconds for this example.
Resize and crop the image so it matches the video exactly.
If the footage fills the page, the still should fill the page in the same way.
Preview the join at normal speed.
If the picture jumps, adjust the split point or capture a closer matching frame.
Keep transitions off at both edges of the still for a clean, sudden freeze.
You can add a label or arrow during the hold if you want to explain something.
Here's the part to check separately.
Inserting a still image does not automatically create the audio treatment you want.
Decide whether speech, music or silence should continue during the hold,
then arrange the audio tracks.
Export as MP4 and check that movement pauses and resumes smoothly.
