This tutorial shows you how to create a simple digital planner in Canva and prepare a downloadable product file.
Start with one useful product for a specific audience.
In this example, create a printable weekly planner for people managing work and personal tasks.
Create an A4 portrait design.
Add a heading called weekly planner, a space for the date, and seven labelled sections for the days of the week.
Use lines or a table to leave room for handwriting.
Add a second page for priorities and tasks.
Include three priority boxes, a checklist and a small section for deadlines.
Add a third page with a notes area and a short weekly review.
Use your own wording and build a consistent layout with matching fonts, spacing and colours.
Make sure writing areas are large enough to use on paper and choose share, download and PDF print.
Download all three pages, open the PDF and print a test copy.
Check the margins, text size and space available for writing.
Create a separate preview image showing the pages and state the included page count and paper size.
Those details help buyers understand exactly what they will receive.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
An editable Canva product needs a template link so each customer can create their own copy.
On an eligible plan, open share, choose template link, create the link and test it.
You can place that link inside a short instruction PDF for delivery.
For products you sell, use an original design and check the permitted uses of any Canva content included.
Keep your editable master and deliver the tested PDF or template instructions through your chosen storefront.
