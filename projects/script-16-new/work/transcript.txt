This tutorial shows you how to move audio to another point in your Canva video, or reuse it in a different Canva design.
For a different moment in the same video, select the audio bar on the timeline.
Drag the middle of the bar left or right until its start lines up with the scene or action you want.
Grab the body of the clip rather than an edge handle.
Dragging an edge trims the audio while dragging the body changes its position.
Play the transition into the new location to check the timing.
To reuse the audio in another design, select the audio clip and copy it.
Use CTRL and C on Windows or CMD and C on a Mac.
Open the destination video design and paste with CTRL and V or CMD and V.
Check where the pasted audio begins, then adjust its position.
If copying is unavailable in that editor layout, add the original file from uploads in the destination design and recreate the selected section.
Here's the part many tutorials skip. A different design may have a different duration.
Check the audio's trim, volume and ending after transferring it so it does not start late or stop before the final scene.
When the destination plays correctly, remove the source clip only if you intended to move it, rather than keep a copy.
