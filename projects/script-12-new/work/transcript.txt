This tutorial shows you how to turn a Canva invitation into a digital invitation that plays music.
Open your invitation and check that the names, date, time and location are correct.
Add any animation you want, keeping the important information easy to read.
Open audio or find the audio category through elements.
Choose a license track that matches the event.
To use your own recording, go to Uploads, upload the audio file and add it to the design.
Set the invitation's duration using its timing or duration control.
For this example, allow around 15 seconds so guests have time to read the details.
If your static design does not expose the timing controls you need, copy its contents into a video design with a matching layout.
Trim the music to the invitation's length.
Preview the opening and ending, then lower the volume if you have also included a spoken message.
A short fade at the end can make the music finish more smoothly.
Here's the part many tutorials skip. A printed card, PNG, JPG or ordinary PDF will not play the soundtrack.
Download the invitation as an MP4 video to include the music in a file you can send.
Open the exported video and check the sound.
Send that MP4 through the messaging app or service you use for the invitations.
