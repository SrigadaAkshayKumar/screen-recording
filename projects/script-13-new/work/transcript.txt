This tutorial shows you how to combine narration, background music and sound effects in one Canva video.
Open a video design and go to Uploads.
Upload your voiceover file and add it to the timeline.
Position it where the narration should begin.
Next, open Audio and choose a licensed music track, or add another uploaded file.
Canva supports layering multiple audio tracks so your music can play underneath the narration.
Add a sound effect in the same way.
Move it to the moment it should accompany, such as a title appearing or a scene changing.
Listen while watching the action so you can adjust the start point precisely.
Select each track separately and set its volume.
Keep the narration clear, lower the background music and make the sound effect only as loud as the moment needs.
The right balance depends on the recordings, so use your ears instead of giving every file the same value.
Trim the clips to remove unwanted openings and endings.
To create a sequence of songs, place them one after another.
To mix them, let their timeline ranges overlap.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Adding a second sound does not automatically replace the first.
Preview every overlap to check for accidental doubled music, or a sound effect covering a spoken word.
Download an MP4 video when the full mix sounds right.
