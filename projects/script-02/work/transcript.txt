This tutorial shows you how to mask a photo or video inside a shape using Canva's frames.
Open your design and select elements from the left sidebar.
Find the frames category and choose a circle, arch, letter or another frame that suits your layout.
Use a frame with a landscape placeholder inside it.
A decorative outline or an ordinary graphic will not automatically crop your image.
Open uploads and upload the photo or video you want to use.
Drag it over the frame until the frame highlights then release it.
The media is now visible only inside that shape.
Double click the image to adjust the crop.
Drag the picture to reposition your subject and use its corner handles to enlarge it.
Click done when the important part is inside the frame.
Resize the entire frame by selecting it once and dragging a corner.
This changes the size of the masked result while keeping its shape.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Eligible Canva shapes can also become frames.
Add a basic shape and your image to the page.
Select both, right click and choose change to frame.
If that option is missing use a ready-made frame instead.
Graphics and shapes with multiple outlines may not qualify.
To try a different picture drag the replacement onto the existing frame.
You can change the image without rebuilding the layout.
Download a still design as PNG or a design containing moving video as MP4.
You have now created a shaped mask in Canva.
