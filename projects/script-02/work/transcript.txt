This tutorial shows you how to design a printable busy book in Canva with matching activities
and a separate page of movable pieces.
Click Create a Design and choose A4 Document or US Letter, depending on your paper.
Start with a blank page or search Canva's templates for an activity worksheet you can customize.
Add a simple title, such as Match the Shapes.
Under Elements add a circle, square and triangle.
Give them a pale fill and a clear outline,
then arrange them with enough space between the targets.
Add a short instruction above the activity and a label underneath each shape.
Keep the instruction large and use the same font throughout the book.
Now duplicate the page.
On the duplicate, delete the title, instructions and labels, leaving the three shapes.
Change those shapes to bright colors and spread them apart.
This becomes the matching piece sheet.
Return to the activity page and lock the target shapes so you do not move them accidentally.
Add more pages for colors, numbers or matching animals.
Duplicate an existing layout to keep the headings and spacing consistent.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Design the targets and movable pieces at the same size.
Create the pieces by duplicating the targets rather than drawing a second set by eye.
Otherwise they may not fit after printing.
Click Share, download and choose PDF Print.
Export both the activity pages and their matching piece sheets.
Print one pair at actual size before printing the entire book.
Check that the pieces match their targets,
then assemble your pages in a folder or binder.
You now have a busy book layout you can expand with more activities.
