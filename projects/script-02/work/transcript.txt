This tutorial shows you how to add a recorded voiceover to a Canva video and generate subtitles from that narration.
Open uploads, select upload files, and choose your voiceover recording.
When the upload finishes, add it to the video and position the audio at the point where the narration should start.
Play the video and adjust the scene lengths to match your voice.
If the original footage also contains sound, lower its volume so it does not compete with the narration.
Next, open text and choose captions under dynamic text.
In the captions panel, use apply captions to to select the voiceover audio.
Leave unrelated music and other recordings out of the selection.
Choose a caption style and select generate captions.
The generated subtitles appear on the video timeline.
Listen to the narration while reading the captions.
Correct names, numbers, and any words can be misunderstood.
Keep the text away from faces and from the area where social media buttons will appear.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Generate subtitles after you finish moving and trimming the voiceover.
Changing the narration timing afterward can leave the subtitles appearing before or after the words are spoken.
If you do adjust the voiceover later, review the affected captions before exporting.
Select share, download, and mp4 video.
Open the downloaded file and check that both the narration and the subtitles are present.
