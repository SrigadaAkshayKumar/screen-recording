This tutorial shows you how to add an animated entrance to a chart in Canva.
Open your presentation and select the slide where you want the chart.
Open Charts from the side panel.
If you do not see it, open Apps and search for Charts.
Add a bar chart, select it and click Edit to enter your own labels and values.
For this example, use three categories so the result is easy to follow.
Finish the chart colours and labels before adding motion.
Leave enough space around it for the title and any notes.
Select the chart and open Animate.
Choose an available chart or element animation, then preview it.
A simple entrance keeps attention on the numbers.
Adjust the animation controls offered for your selected chart and effect.
If your chart type does not offer the animation you need, use an image version instead.
Put the finished chart on its own page.
Download that page as PNG, upload the PNG into Canva and animate it as an image.
Keep the original editable chart on a separate page for future changes.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Moving or fading a chart as one object is different from animating individual bars or changes in its data.
The image method animates the whole chart and does not create a live data animation.
Preview the slide in presentation mode.
To share the movement as a file, download the animated page as MP4 Video.
