This tutorial shows you how to create a complete magazine in Canva, including the cover, article pages and a downloadable PDF.
Start by deciding the finished page size.
For this example, create an A4 portrait design.
If a printer will produce the magazine, use that printer's required dimensions.
Search Canva's templates for magazine layouts.
Choose a design with suitable interior pages, as well as a cover.
Apply the pages you need or add pages individually to your existing design.
Build a simple structure, a cover, a contents page, your articles and a back cover.
Replace the magazine name, issue date, cover image and article headlines.
Open uploads to add your photos.
Drag a photo into a frame, then double-click it to adjust the crop.
Keep the subject clear of headlines and page edges.
For an article, add a heading and two separate body text boxes to create columns.
Paste the first section into the left column and continue the article in the right.
Use the same body font, text size and spacing throughout.
Duplicate a finished article page to keep the layout consistent.
Replace its content and update the page number.
Use grid view to drag the pages into order, then check that the contents page lists the correct locations.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Separate text boxes need their own content.
When an article grows, manually move the extra text into the next column or page and check the reading order.
Shrinking the entire article can make it difficult to read.
Choose share, download and PDF standard for a digital copy, or PDF print for a print copy.
Add bleed and crop marks only when the printer requests them.
Export the pages in reading order and inspect the PDF before sharing it.
