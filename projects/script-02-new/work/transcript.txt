This tutorial shows you how to add a subtle text or logo watermark to your video in Canva.
Open your video design.
To use a logo, go to uploads and add your logo file.
To use text, open text, add a text box and type your channel name, website or business name.
Resize the watermark and place it where it remains readable without covering the main subject.
A corner is a useful starting point but check whether your captions or the platform's on-screen buttons will overlap it.
Select the watermark, click transparency in the toolbar and lower the value.
Try around 40 to 60 as a starting point, then preview it over both light and dark parts of your footage.
A value of 100 makes the element fully visible, while 0 hides it.
Use position and layers if you need to bring the watermark above the video.
Adjust its timeline layer so it covers the intended section and add it to other scenes when necessary.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A watermark that looks clear over one frame may disappear over another.
Test a white version, a dark version or a small contrasting backing until it stays visible during playback.
Finish by downloading the design as an mp4 video.
Check the saved file to confirm the watermark remains visible through the final frame.
