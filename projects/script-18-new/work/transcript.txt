This tutorial shows you how to make music fade in gradually and fade out smoothly in a Canva video.
Open your video project. Select the audio track in the timeline and click fade in the toolbar.
Adjust the fade in and fade out durations using the controls that appear.
Start with about one second at the beginning and two seconds at the end.
These are only starting points.
Listen and shorten or lengthen the fades until they suit your soundtrack.
A fade in increases the sound gradually from silence while a fade out reduces it before the clip ends.
Position and trim the audio first so those changes happen at the correct moments in your video.
You can also fade the sound directly inside a video clip.
Select that video, open audio tools and adjust its fade in or fade out controls.
Current Canva supports this without requiring you to extract the soundtrack first.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A long fade can make the first or last spoken words difficult to hear.
For narration, keep the fade inside any available silence or use a much shorter duration.
Play the opening, the ending and any joins between audio sections.
Check that the music does not drop out before your final visual has finished.
