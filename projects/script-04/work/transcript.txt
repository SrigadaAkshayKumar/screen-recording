This tutorial shows you how to animate a shape in Canva without animating the entire page.
Open your design, go to Elements, and add a basic shape.
For this example, choose a circle.
Set its color, resize it, and position it near the center of the canvas.
Select the circle and click Animate.
Open the Element tab so the effect applies to the shape.
Preview the available styles and choose a simple entrance, such as Rise or Fade.
If direction controls appear, choose where the shape should come from.
Adjust the speed or duration controls available for that effect.
Play the design to see the result.
If the movement feels too strong, switch to a more subtle animation rather than making the shape harder to follow.
To make two shapes move together, select both while holding Shift and click Group.
Then animate the group.
To give them different entrances, keep them separate and animate each one individually.
In a video design, use their timeline layers to stagger when the shapes appear.
Try introducing the circle first and a second shape one second later.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Grouped shapes share the group's movement.
If you need independent motion or timing, ungroup them before adjusting the individual shapes.
