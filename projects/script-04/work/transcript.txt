This tutorial shows you how to add a sound effect in Canva and time it to a specific action in your video.
Move the timeline playhead to the moment where the sound should begin.
Open audio in the editor or use add audio on the video timeline.
Browse the sound effects or search for a description such as whoosh, click, applause or notification.
If audio is hidden in your layout, open apps to find it or look under audio in elements.
Preview an effect, then add it to the design.
You can also open uploads and add a sound effect you already have permission to use.
Find the new audio clip on the timeline, drag it into position so the sound matches the action,
such as the instant a title lands or a button is clicked.
Trim any unnecessary silence from the effect.
Select the audio and lower its volume if it is much louder than the rest of the video.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Align the noticeable sound in the recording with the action,
rather than automatically aligning the beginning of the file.
A whoosh may contain a quiet lead-in before its strongest moment.
Replay a few seconds before and after the effect.
Listen with the narration and music playing so you can judge the actual mix.
When the timing sounds right, export as an MP4 video to keep the sound in the finished design.
