This tutorial shows you how to connect your Canva account to ChatGPT and open a generated design for further editing.
Sign in to ChatGPT in your desktop browser, open Settings, then Plugins.
Some interfaces may show apps instead.
Search for Canva and select its official listing.
If an install option appears, install the plugin, then select Connect and follow the sign-in process.
Choose the Canva account that contains the designs you want to use, review the requested access, and complete the authorization.
Return to ChatGPT and confirm that Canva appears connected.
Open a new conversation, type the AT symbol, and choose Canva from the available options.
You can also select it through the plus menu when that option is shown.
Give it a specific request.
For example, ask Canva to create a five-slide presentation about a weekend trip, using a blue and white style, a title slide, a packing list, an itinerary, a budget, and a final checklist.
Review the return design options and choose one.
Open the design in Canva using the provided link or editing button.
Replace sample text, adjust the layout, and check that the content matches your request.
When the design is ready, use Canva's share and download controls to export it in the format you need.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Connecting the wrong Canva account can make your existing projects appear to be missing.
Check the connected account before trying to find a particular design.
If Canva is unavailable or marked as disabled by an administrator, check your workspace's app permissions.
If a previously working connection fails, disconnect Canva and connect the correct account again.
You can now request supported Canva design actions directly from your chat GPT conversation.
