This tutorial shows you how to upload an image to Canva, place it in a design, and adjust how it looks.
Open your design and select Uploads in the left sidebar.
Click Upload Files, choose the image, and click Open.
Wait for its thumbnail to finish loading.
Then click the thumbnail to add the image to your page.
Drag the image to the position you want.
Use a corner handle to make it larger or smaller while keeping its proportions.
To crop the image, select it and click Crop or double-click it.
Adjust the visible area, then click Done or click outside the image.
If you want the photo inside a particular shape, open Elements, find a frame and add it to the page.
Drag your uploaded image into the frame.
Double-click the image inside the frame to reposition the photo.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Cropping hides part of the image. It does not create extra detail.
If the original photo is small, enlarging it too much can make the result look blurry.
For basic corrections, select the image, open Edit and use the available adjustment controls.
Make a small change and compare it with the original appearance.
When you're finished, choose Share, then download and select the image format you need.
Your image is now uploaded, positioned and ready to use.
