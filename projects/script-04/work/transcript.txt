This tutorial shows you how to design a soap label in Canva and prepare a correctly sized file for printing.
First, measure the area your label should cover.
For this example, we will make a front label that is 70mm wide and 40mm high.
In Canva, click Create a Design, choose Custom Size, switch the units to millimeters, and enter 70 by 40.
Create the blank design.
Open Design and search for soap label layouts or build your own using text and a few simple elements.
Add your brand name at the top, the soap name in the center, and the scent underneath.
Upload your logo through uploads and place it where it does not compete with the product name.
Use one main color and a second accent color.
A single botanical illustration can add detail without filling the label with decoration.
Add the actual product information you intend to print, including the net weight and your prepared ingredient text.
If the information needs more room, make a separate back label instead of squeezing everything onto the front.
Keep small text away from the edges.
For professional printing, check the printer's bleed requirements and extend any edge-to-edge background into the bleed area.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A label that looks large on a monitor can be tiny on the product.
Print a paper proof at 100%, cut it out, and place it on the soap packaging before ordering labels.
Click Share, download, and choose PDF Print.
Include crop marks and bleed only when your printer requests them.
You now have a reusable soap label design at the intended physical size.
