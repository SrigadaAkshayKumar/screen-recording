This tutorial shows you how to create a simple logo in Canva and export it for use in your
other designs.
From Canva's homepage, search for logo, start with a blank design, or choose a template
with a layout close to the style you want.
Replace the sample business name with your own.
Pick a clear font and adjust the size until the name is easy to read.
Use a second font only when it adds a clear purpose, such as a short tagline, open elements
to add a simple shape, or use uploads to bring in your own symbol.
Resize it and place it above or beside the name.
Select the objects and use position to align them.
Choose one main colour and, if needed, one supporting colour.
Duplicate the page to test a dark version, a light version, and a version containing only
the symbol.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A logo that looks good while zoomed in may become unreadable as a small profile picture.
Reduce the editor zoom and check whether the name, spacing and symbol are still recognisable.
Remove tiny details that disappear.
When you are happy with the result, select Share, Download and PNG.
If your plan includes transparent downloads, enable Transparent Background so the logo can
sit cleanly over other colours.
Otherwise export it on your chosen solid background.
Keep the Canva design so you can adjust the original text and shapes later.
Your logo and its alternate versions are now ready to use.
