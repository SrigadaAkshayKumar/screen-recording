This tutorial shows you how to download the invoice for a Canva subscription or purchase as a PDF.
Start on the Canva homepage and check that you are signed into the account used for the payment.
If you work in several teams, switch to the team where the purchase was made.
Open your profile menu and choose Settings.
Then select Orders and Invoices.
Find the transaction you need.
Use the date or purchase type filters to narrow the list or search for an invoice number when you have one.
Check the payment date and amount before downloading.
This helps you avoid saving the invoice for the wrong renewal or print order.
Select Download Invoice beside the transaction.
In some layouts, open the three-dot menu first.
Another layout labels the Action View invoice, which also downloads the PDF.
Open the downloaded file and check the invoice number, billing details and total.
Give the file a useful name containing the purchase date so you can find it later.
Here's the main troubleshooting point.
Purchases billed through Apple or Google may need their invoice or receipt from that provider.
A payment made through an app store will not necessarily appear like a direct Canva website purchase.
If the transaction is missing, check the Canva confirmation email and verify the account and team again.
Team permissions can also affect which invoices you can see.
You can now store the PDF with your purchase records or attach it wherever the invoice is needed.
