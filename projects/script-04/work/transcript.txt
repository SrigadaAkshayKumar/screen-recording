This tutorial shows you how to download a Canva video to your computer as an MP4 file.
Open the design and preview it from the beginning.
Check that the video clips have finished uploading and that the text, music and final scene play correctly.
Click Share in the upper right corner, then choose Download.
Open the File Type menu and select MP4 Video.
If your design offers a page selection menu, choose the pages you want included.
Select all pages when the finished video should contain the entire sequence.
In a continuous video timeline, the download uses the video sequence rather than the older page-based export workflow.
Choose an available quality setting if your account offers one.
If you see a notice about paid content, replace that content or use a plan or license that covers it before downloading.
Click Download and wait for Canva to finish processing.
Your browser will save the file to its normal download location.
Or ask you where to put it.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A Canva sharing link is different from a downloaded video file.
If you need to upload the video to YouTube or send it as an attachment, use the actual MP4 from your downloads folder.
Open that file in a video player and check the first scene, the ending and the sound.
If download is unavailable in a design someone shared with you, ask the owner to check your access and download permissions.
Your Canva video is now saved as a playable file on your device.
