This tutorial shows you how to export a Canva video without the sound recorded in the original clip.
Open a video design and add your clip from uploads.
Select the video on the canvas or in the timeline so its editing controls appear.
Click the volume control and set the video sound to zero, or use the mute button.
Play the clip to confirm that its recorded sound is now silent.
If the design includes several videos, check each clip that should be silent.
An adjustment to one video does not guarantee the other clips have the same setting.
Look for separate audio bars in the timeline as well.
Background music, voiceovers and previously extracted sound can still play after the video itself is muted.
Select and delete any of those tracks you also want to remove.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The video's volume control silences its whole soundtrack, including any voice and music already mixed together in that source.
It does not isolate one sound from the others.
You can now add a replacement voiceover or license music, or leave the design completely silent.
Preview the entire project to check the result.
Choose Share, Download and MP4 Video.
Open the downloaded file and listen again.
Your new export uses the sound settings you applied, while the original file on your computer remains available.
