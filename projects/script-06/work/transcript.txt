This tutorial shows you how to download a Canva design as a PNG with a transparent background.
Open the design and check what sits behind the artwork.
Delete any full page photograph, texture or coloured rectangle that you do not want included.
Set the page background to white for this export workflow.
Click Share, choose Download and select PNG from the file type menu.
Enable transparent background.
This download option requires an eligible paid or organisation plan such as Canva Pro or Education.
Select the page you want, choose an appropriate export size if available and click Download.
Now test the actual file.
Upload the PNG into a new Canva design and place it over a dark rectangle.
You should see the new background around the logo instead of a white box.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Transparent background removes the page background but it does not erase white pixels baked into an uploaded image.
If your logo arrived as a JPG with a white rectangle around it, that rectangle is part of the picture.
Remove the image background with an appropriate background removal tool or upload an original transparent logo then export again.
Also inspect the design for decorative objects behind the logo.
A large white shape is still a shape and can remain visible even when the page itself exports transparently.
If a file viewer displays the finished PNG on white, do not assume the transparency failed.
The dark background test is more useful than the viewer's default appearance.
Your PNG is now ready to place over photos, videos and other coloured designs.
