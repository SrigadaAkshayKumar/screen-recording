This tutorial shows you how to create printable wall art in Canva and export a file at the correct physical size.
Before designing, choose the finished print dimensions.
For this example, select Custom Size, change the units to inches, and enter 12 inches wide by 18 inches high.
Choose a background colour, then add your artwork through uploads or build a design with text and graphics.
For a simple typography print, add a short quote, select a readable font, and adjust the line spacing.
Use position to centre the main elements.
Keep important text comfortably inside the edges, especially if the artwork will sit behind a frame opening or mat.
If the background should reach the cut edge, open file, settings, and show print bleed, extend the background through the bleed area.
Confirm the printer's requirements before including bleed in the final file.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A larger canvas cannot restore detail to a small, uploaded photo.
At 300 pixels per inch, a 12 by 18 inch photo needs about 3,600 by 5,400 pixels.
Use a sufficiently large original image and inspect it closely before printing.
Click Share, then Download, and choose PDF Print.
Enable crop marks and bleed only if your print provider asks for them.
Use the colour profile requested by the printer when that option is available.
Open the exported PDF and check its dimensions.
Print a test or order a proof before producing a batch.
If the artwork includes a deliberate border, check the print dialogue scaling so the border remains even.
For additional sizes, create versions with the correct proportions and check the composition again.
Different aspect ratios may need their own layout adjustments.
