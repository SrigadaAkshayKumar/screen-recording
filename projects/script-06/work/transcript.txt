This tutorial shows you how to repeat a music clip so it covers a longer Canva video.
Add the music from audio or from your uploaded files.
Place the first clip at the beginning of the section where you want the background music.
Trim it to the musical section you want to repeat.
Listen to the beginning and end of that section before making copies.
Add the same music again from audio or uploads.
If you trimmed the first clip, adjust the second one to use the same portion of the recording.
Drag the second clip directly after the first.
Zoom into the timeline to check that there is no accidental gap or overlap between them.
Continue adding copies until the music covers the required duration.
Trim the last copy so it ends with your video, then add a short fade out if the ending sounds abrupt.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Repeating a file does not guarantee a seamless musical loop.
A recording with a fading ending, a silent opening, or an unfinished phrase can sound awkward
every time it restarts.
Choose a section that begins and ends on compatible beats or use music made for looping.
Listen across each join rather than checking only the first copy.
Preview the complete video and download it as an mp4.
The exported music repeats for the duration you created on the timeline.
