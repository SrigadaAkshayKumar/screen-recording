This tutorial shows you how to animate an existing logo in Canva for a short intro or social media video.
Create a video design in Canva.
Open uploads, click upload files and choose your logo.
A PNG with a transparent background works well when you want the logo to sit cleanly over a coloured background.
Add the logo to the canvas, resize it and centre it.
Choose a background colour that makes the logo easy to read.
Select the logo and click animate.
Choose a suitable entrance such as fade for a restrained reveal or pop for a more energetic introduction.
Preview the available options before selecting one.
Adjust any speed or duration settings the effect provides.
Give the logo enough time to remain visible after its entrance so viewers can recognise it.
If you built the logo from editable text and shapes inside Canva, group the parts to animate them together.
Keep the parts separate when you want the symbol to appear first and the brand name to follow.
Use the video timeline to set the logo's visible duration.
Add a tagline as a separate text box if you want an additional message underneath it.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
An uploaded PNG is one image.
Canva cannot independently animate the letters or symbol inside that flattened file.
Use separate assets or editable Canva objects for separate movements.
Download as MP4 video and check the finished intro before adding it to your main video.
