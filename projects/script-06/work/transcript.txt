This tutorial shows you how to create a reusable daily planner in Canva with a schedule, priorities, and a simple task list.
Start a new A4 or US letter design.
Search the templates for daily planner, then choose a layout with the sections you actually need.
You can also start with a blank page, add a heading at the top, and leave a space for the date.
Keep the date blank if you want to reuse the same page throughout the year.
Divide the page into two main areas.
Use the wider area for your schedule and the smaller area for priorities and tasks.
Add a short top three section so your most important jobs are easy to see.
For the schedule, add a row of time labels and horizontal writing lines.
Duplicate the first line to keep their style consistent, then select the lines and use position to distribute them evenly.
Create the task list with small square outlines beside each writing line.
Add a notes box at the bottom for reminders that do not belong at a particular time.
Use a light background, dark text, and subtle divider lines.
Leave enough room to write by hand rather than filling the page with decorative graphics.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Build one complete writing row before duplicating it.
Group its checkbox and line together so the spacing remains consistent when you move or copy the row.
Download the planner as PDF print and print a test page at actual size.
Check that your handwriting fits comfortably between the lines.
Adjust the spacing if necessary, then save the Canva design as your reusable Planner Master.
