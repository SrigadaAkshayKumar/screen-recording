This tutorial shows you how to add a working navigation menu to a Canva website and customize
its appearance.
Open a website design with the pages you want visitors to navigate between.
Use short, clear names so the menu is easy to scan.
Select Publish Website and open Settings.
Turn on Navigation Menu.
Canva adds the navigation bar to the design where you can select it and adjust its appearance.
Click the menu and use its toolbar to choose the font, text size, background colour and
text colour.
Keep the text readable against the background and leave enough space between items.
Choose Edit on the menu, then open the Items tab.
Hide any items you do not want in the navigation.
To include an outside destination, choose Add Link and enter its label and web address.
In the Menu Settings tab, you can show a logo, upload your own and give it a link back to
the home page.
To change a pages menu label, open the website's publishing settings, select Pages and edit
that pages menu label.
Use a short label such as Work even if the page's full title is longer.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Typing a row of words at the top of a website does not automatically turn those words into
working navigation.
Use the navigation menu controls and test the destinations in a preview.
Check the website on a narrow mobile preview as well as desktop.
Publish or republish the site, then open the live address and test every menu item.
