This tutorial shows you how to create a reusable text or logo watermark in Canva.
Open the photo or design you want to watermark.
Press T to add a text box, then type your name, website or brand name.
Choose a clear font and a colour that remains visible against the picture.
White often works on darker areas, while a dark grey can work on light backgrounds.
If you already have a logo, open uploads and add that instead.
A logo with a transparent background is easier to place because it does not bring a solid rectangle into the photo.
Position the watermark where it will be visible without covering the most important detail.
Select it, open transparency and reduce the value until the image underneath remains clear.
Start around 30% and adjust for the actual picture.
For a diagonal watermark, use the rotation handle and place the text across the centre.
For a simple signature, keep it smaller near a corner.
To reuse the watermark, copy the text or logo into another Canva design.
You can also keep a separate design containing your watermark versions in light and dark colours.
If you need a reusable image file, place only the watermark on a separate page.
Choose Share and Download, select PNG and enable Transparent Background.
