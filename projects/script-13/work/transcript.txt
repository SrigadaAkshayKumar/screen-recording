This tutorial shows you how to create a bold 3D style text effect in Canva by stacking
slightly offset copies.
Open your design and add a text box.
Type a short word and choose a thick, bold font.
This method works best when the letters have enough weight to show the depth clearly.
Set the original text to a bright colour.
This will be the front face of the finished effect.
Duplicate the text using Control and D on Windows, or Command and D on a Mac.
Change this copy to a darker colour.
Open position, move it behind the original, and offset it slightly down and to the right.
Duplicate that darker copy.
Place the new copy another small step down and to the right, then move it behind the previous layer.
Repeat several times using a consistent offset.
The overlapping dark copies should form one continuous edge rather than several separate shadows.
If the front face becomes covered, open position, then layers, and move the original bright text to the top.
Adjust the background colour until both the front face and the dark edge are easy to see.
Select all the text layers and group them so you can move and resize the finished effect together.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Every copy is still a separate text box.
If you change the word later, you must update all the copies or the old letters will remain visible in the extrusion.
Finish the wording before building the stack.
This creates a flat graphic with a convincing 3D appearance.
Download it as a PNG or keep the grouped layers in Canva for future editing.
