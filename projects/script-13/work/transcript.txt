This tutorial shows you how to create a table of contents in Canva and make its entries clickable in a PDF.
Open your finished document and add a blank page near the front.
Use the page thumbnails or grid view to move it into the right position.
Add the heading contents.
Create a text box for your first chapter title and another for its page number.
Align the title to the left and the number to the right.
Select the two boxes, group them, and duplicate the row for your remaining chapters.
Replace each title and number, then space the rows evenly using position.
Use the same fonts and colours as the rest of the document.
If you include subheadings, make them slightly smaller and indent them beneath the main chapter.
To make an entry clickable, select its title, open link, and choose the destination from pages in this document.
Repeat for the other chapters.
You can also add a small, back-to-contents link on each chapter's opening page.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The number printed on a page may differ from its position in the Canva file.
For example, a cover can be the first PDF page without displaying the number one.
Make the visible content's numbers match your chosen numbering system
and make each link point to the actual chapter page.
Click Share, download, and choose PDF Standard.
Leave flattened PDF turned off so the links remain interactive.
Open the downloaded PDF and test every entry.
After adding, removing, or rearranging pages later,
review both the displayed numbers and link destinations before exporting again.
