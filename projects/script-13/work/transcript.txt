This tutorial shows you how to make an animation move faster or slower in Canva.
Open your design and select the animated object.
Click Animate in the toolbar, then select its applied animation to view the available controls.
Some animations offer speed presets such as Slow, Medium and Fast.
Choose Slow for a more gradual entrance or Fast for a quicker reveal, then play the preview.
Other supported effects provide intro and outro duration controls.
A shorter intro duration makes the entrance happen more quickly.
A longer duration spreads the same entrance over more time.
If the object uses Create and Animation, open its custom path settings and adjust the animation speed slider.
Preview the entire path after each change so the object reaches its intended destination at a useful pace.
Different effects provide different controls.
If your selected animation does not offer a speed adjustment,
choose another effect that does rather than looking for one universal setting for every animation.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Extending the object's timeline layer controls how long it remains visible.
That is separate from the speed of its entrance or custom movement.
Adjust speed in animate and adjust visibility on the timeline.
For text, make sure the faster setting still leaves the message readable.
For images, watch for important details moving off the canvas.
Preview the design from the beginning, then export as MP4 video or GIF to keep the adjusted motion.
