This tutorial shows you how to change a recorded voice using a voice changing app inside Canva.
Prepare a short recording of your own voice and save a copy of the original.
A recording with little background noise gives the app a better starting point.
Open a video design in Canva, select Apps and search for AI Voice Changer.
Open the app listing and review its available voices, account requirements and usage allowance.
Upload your audio file into the app.
The voice changer described by Canva supports formats including MP3, WAV, M4A and MP4.
Although you should check the specific app you open.
Choose a replacement voice and generate a short test.
Listen for understandable words, natural pauses and any distortion on louder sounds.
If the result is too extreme, choose a less dramatic voice or adjust any controls the app provides.
Keep the original recording available so you can compare both versions.
Once you're satisfied, add the changed voice to your design.
Align the audio with the correct scene, trim unwanted silence and adjust the volume.
If the original video already contains your speech, mute or reduce that original audio so both voices do not play together.
Here's the important distinction.
Changing a recording is different from choosing a text to speech voice.
A voice changer processes audio you supply.
AI Voice generates speech from written text.
Preview the complete scene and check that the replacement remains synchronized with the visuals.
Download the finished video as MP4, then play the exported file to confirm that only the intended voice is audible.
