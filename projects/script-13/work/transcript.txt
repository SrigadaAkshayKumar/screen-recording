This tutorial shows you how to make a YouTube channel banner in Canva and check that its important details survive different screencrops.
Search Canva for YouTube banner and open a template or create a custom design at 2560 by 1440 pixels.
That is YouTube's recommended banner size. Choose a background that can extend across the entire canvas.
Upload your own image if needed, then adjust its crop so it still looks good when only the centre is visible.
Add your channel name in a large clear font, under it place a short line explaining what the channel offers such as simple video editing tutorials.
Keep the name, logo and essential message together near the centre.
The full image is visible on television while smaller screens show a narrower section.
Use the outer areas for background decoration.
Use position to align your text and graphics.
Keep the layout simple enough to understand quickly and check that the lettering has strong contrast against the background.
Download the banner through Share and Download.
Use PNG or JPG and keep the file within YouTube's 6MB banner limit.
If a PNG is too large, try a JPG export.
Open YouTube Studio and go to Customisation, then Profile.
Under Banner Image, upload the file or choose Change to replace an existing banner.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The upload preview is the final crop check.
Inspect every device area before accepting it.
If part of your channel name falls outside the smallest visible area, return to Canva and move the important elements closer to the centre.
Apply the crop and publish or save the channel changes.
Your new banner is now ready to introduce the channel.
