This tutorial shows you how to create a closer crop of a video in Canva and use it as a punch-in during an edit.
Open a video design, upload your footage and add it to the timeline.
Select the video on the canvas and choose Crop or double-click it to open the crop controls.
Inside the crop area, enlarge the footage using its corner handles and drag it until the subject is in the right position.
Keep the canvas filled so you do not create empty edges.
Click Done to apply the Closer view.
Play the clip and check the subject throughout the shot.
If the person moves out of frame, use a smaller crop or adjust the section you are using.
For a punch-in partway through the video, first undo the crop so the opening is wide again.
Move the playhead to the moment you want the view to change. Right. Click the clip and split it.
Select the section after the cut and apply the tighter crop to that section only.
Split again where you want the wide view to return, then reset the crop on the following section.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Increasing the editor's zoom percentage only makes your workspace preview larger.
It does not zoom the exported video.
