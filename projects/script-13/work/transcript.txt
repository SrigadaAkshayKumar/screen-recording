This tutorial shows you how to change a captions font in Canva and keep the words readable after the change.
Open your video and move to a moment where the caption is visible.
Select the caption text, then click the font name on the toolbar.
Search for a font or browse the list.
Choose a clear typeface such as a simple sans serif and click it to apply it to the selected caption.
Check the size and weight after changing the font.
Two fonts at the same size can take up very different amounts of space,
so adjust the text box and font size if the line becomes crowded.
Replay the caption over different parts of the footage.
Use a contrasting colour or text effect if the new font is hard to read against the background.
For a consistent look across generated captions, open captions, then styles,
and choose a caption style with a suitable font.
A style pack applies across the video's captions.
Individual toolbar changes are for the selected caption.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The best looking font in a large editor preview may become difficult to read on a phone.
Check a smaller preview and look for letters that are easy to distinguish quickly.
Also review longer lines because changing fonts can create new line breaks.
Download the video as an MP4 and check the final caption appearance.
