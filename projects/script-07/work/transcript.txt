This tutorial shows you how to upload an audio file to Canva and add it to a video design.
Open your video design and select Uploads from the left sidebar.
Click Upload Files.
Choose the audio file and click Open.
Wait for the upload to finish, then look under Audio in the Uploads panel.
Click the file to add it to the design.
If your video editor displays Add Audio on the timeline,
you can also use that button to select your uploaded audio.
The audio appears on its own track.
Drag the entire track left or right to choose when it starts.
To shorten the track, select it and drag an edge inward.
To change its loudness, keep the track selected,
open the volume control and adjust the slider.
Press play and listen to the result.
If your video already contains sound,
check that the two tracks work together instead of competing with each other.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Adding audio to a design does not make a PNG, JPG or ordinary PDF download carry sound.
Choose MP4 Video when you want the exported design to include the audio.
Also check the end of the timeline.
Make sure the visuals last for the section of audio you want viewers to hear.
Select Share, Download and MP4 Video, then download and test the finished file.
Your uploaded audio is now part of the video.
