This tutorial shows you how to add captions manually in Canva, including when you need wording that is not generated from speech.
Open your video design and play the first section.
Pause where the first caption should appear.
Select text, then add a text box.
Type the caption you want viewers to read.
This could be spoken dialogue, an explanation of the action, or a sound description such as door closes.
Choose a clear font, adjust the size, and position the text where it is easy to see.
Add a contrasting text background or shadow when the footage makes the words difficult to read.
Find the text element on the video timeline.
Move it to the caption's start time, then drag its edges to control when it appears and disappears.
Add another text box for the next caption, or duplicate the first text element and replace its words.
Place each caption at the correct time and preview the change between them.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Shortening a text element controls its time on screen.
It does not change the speed of a text animation.
Give viewers enough time to read the words before the element ends.
Keep each caption short enough to read comfortably, especially on a phone.
Avoid leaving two unrelated captions visible at the same time.
Watch the full video without sound to check readability, then download it as an MP4 video.
