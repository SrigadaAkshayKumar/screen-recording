This tutorial shows you how to create a semi-donut chart in Canva using a full donut chart and a hidden filler segment.
Start on a blank white page.
Open Elements, go to Charts, and add a donut chart.
Select it and click Edit to replace the sample data.
For a 70% progress chart, enter three categories, complete with a value of 70, remaining with 30, and hidden with 100.
Delete any unused sample rows.
The hidden value equals the sum of the real values, so it occupies exactly half of the full circle.
The other half contains your 70 to 30 split.
Change complete to your main colour, remaining to light grey, and hidden to white, matching the page background.
In the chart's customised panel, hide the legend and automatic data labels.
To orient the chart without depending on chart-specific rotation controls, download just this chart page as a PNG.
Upload that PNG into a second white page and rotate the image until the coloured half forms an arch across the top.
Add a separate text box inside the arch and type 70%.
Put a short title above it, an optional zero, and 100 labels at the endpoints.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Canva calculates automatic percentages from the entire dataset, including the hidden 100.
That would label 70 as 35%.
Use your own text label to show the percentage of the visible half.
Keep the original editable chart page.
To change the result later, update complete and remaining so they still total 100, export that page again, and replace the picture.
You now have a semi-donut graphic ready for a presentation, report, or infographic.
