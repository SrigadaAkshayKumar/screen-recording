This tutorial shows you how to build a short ebook in Canva and export it as a PDF your readers can download.
Prepare your text and chapter outline first, then search Canva for ebook templates,
and choose a multi-page design with a page size that suits your readers.
For this example, use a portrait layout with a cover, a contents page, three short chapters, and a final resources page.
Replace the cover title, subtitle, and author name.
Select a clear cover image and keep the title readable when the cover appears small.
Choose one chapter layout and customize its heading, body text, and page number.
Duplicate that page to create the remaining chapters.
Paste your writing in manageable sections so each page stays readable.
Use a consistent body font and generous spacing.
Add images only when they explain something or improve the reading experience.
Check that every image has enough detail for the size at which it appears.
To add a clickable resource, select the relevant text, open the link control, and enter the web address.
You can also link contents entries to pages within the design when those page link options are available.
Open grid view, put the pages in order, and update the contents page.
Read through the ebook to catch missing text, repeated paragraphs, and leftover template content.
Choose Share, Download, and PDF Standard.
Leave Flatten PDF disabled to preserve clickable links.
Download all pages, then open the PDF and test its links.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
This workflow creates a PDF with a fixed page layout.
Text stays where you placed it.
If your publishing platform requires a re-flowable ebook,
prepare and validate a suitable file for that platform separately.
Keep the Canva project as your editable master,
so future corrections can be exported as an updated PDF.
