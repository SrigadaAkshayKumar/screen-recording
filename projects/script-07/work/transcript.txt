This tutorial shows you how to divide one image into three matching panels in Canva while keeping the picture aligned.
Create a custom size design measuring 1500 by 500 pixels.
This will become three square panels, each 500 pixels wide.
Upload your image and set it as the background.
Double-click it to adjust the crop until the complete composition looks right across the wide canvas.
Download that completed page as a PNG.
This gives you one flattened image with the exact proportions needed for the split.
Create a second design measuring 500 by 500 pixels.
Upload the wide PNG and add it as a normal image, keeping its aspect ratio locked.
Open position and set the image width to 1500 pixels.
Its height should be 500 pixels.
Align it to the top of the page, then move it horizontally until the left third fills the square.
Duplicate the page twice.
On the second page, shift the image left by 500 pixels.
On the third, shift it left by 1000 pixels from its original position.
Use position's numeric controls or rulers and guides for accuracy.
Download all three pages as PNGs.
You now have the left, middle and right sections as separate files.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Adding the same picture to three separate frames can create three different automatic crops.
Using one flattened master image and keeping its scale unchanged preserves the continuity between panels.
Place the exported files next to each other to check the joins before uploading or printing them.
