This tutorial shows you how to make a Canva AI voiceover sound more natural by improving the script and voice choice.
Open a video or presentation, select Apps, and search for AI Voice.
Open Canva's AI Voice tool and paste a short section of your narration.
Choose the correct language and a voice style that fits the content.
For a tutorial, start with a clear, conversational voice rather than a dramatic delivery.
Before generating, read the text aloud.
Replace long sentences with shorter ones and remove phrases you would not naturally say in conversation.
Use full stops and commas where a speaker should pause.
Write unusual abbreviations and numbers in the form you want spoken.
For example, write 1.5 if a numeric expression keeps being pronounced incorrectly.
Generate the short sample and listen with headphones.
Pay attention to names, emphasis, and the space between instructions.
If a line sounds awkward, rewrite that line and generate another sample.
Changing the wording often produces a better result than repeatedly generating the exact same sentence.
Once the sample sounds right, generate the remaining sections using the same voice.
Add the narration to the timeline and arrange the sections in order.
Here's the part people often miss.
A voiceover needs room to breathe between screen actions.
Move or trim the audio so each instruction lands when the viewer can follow it.
Keep background music below the narration and preview the complete video.
Listen for abrupt joins, repeated words, and sudden volume changes before exporting.
