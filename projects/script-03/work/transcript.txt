This tutorial shows you how to turn one Canva design into nine connected Instagram posts.
First, choose the shape of your individual posts.
For this example, each portrait tile will be 1080 by 1440 pixels.
Create a master design measuring 3240 by 4320 pixels, which fits three tiles across and three down.
Open file, rulers and guides, and add guides.
Choose a 3x3 grid with no gaps.
These guides show where your artwork will be divided.
Add your background, photos and text across the master design.
Keep important words and faces inside individual tiles so they remain understandable when someone opens just one post.
Download the finished master as a PNG.
Create another Canva design at the individual tile size, 1080 by 1440 pixels.
Open apps, search for image splitter, and select the app by Cootsoft.
Load the exported master image and choose a split with three columns and three rows.
Add the resulting tiles to separate pages, checking that each page contains one full tile rather than the entire master image.
Download all nine pages as PNG files and keep their positions clearly labelled.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Your uploaded image dimensions and Instagram's profile previews may use different crops.
Check the current profile preview before publishing the full grid.
A continuous design can lose alignment when tiles are cropped or posts are pinned or rearranged.
For a profile displaying the newest post first, publish from the bottom right tile back to the top left tile.
Your nine posts will then build the intended grid.
