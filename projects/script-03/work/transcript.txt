This tutorial shows you how to animate a single Canva page while leaving the other pages as they are.
Open your design and select the page you want to animate using its thumbnail.
For this example, choose the second page.
Click an empty area of that page so you are not selecting a text box, photo or shape.
Then click animate in the toolbar and open the page tab.
Hover over the available page animations to preview them.
Choose a simple effect, such as fade, or another page animation that suits your design.
Adjust any direction, speed or duration settings offered for that effect.
Keep apply to all pages off if that option appears, because this tutorial only changes the selected page.
Now preview the presentation from the first page, move to the second page to see the animation,
then continue to the third page to check the result.
If you want only one photo or heading to move on that page,
select that object and use its element or text animation settings instead of the page tab.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A page animation and a transition between pages are separate effects.
If the change between slides still moves unexpectedly,
inspect the transition between those two pages as well.
Use present to play the design inside Canva,
or download it as mp4 video to retain the animation in a video file.
You have now animated one selected page.
