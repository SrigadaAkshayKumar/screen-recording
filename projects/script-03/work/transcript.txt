This tutorial shows you how to prepare the bleed area on a Canva business card before printing.
Open your business card design and confirm that its finished dimensions match the size requested by your printer.
The finished size is the card after trimming.
Open File, then Rulers and Guides and enable Show Print Bleed.
Turn on Show Margins as well.
Now check every edge of the card.
Extend background colours, photos and decorative shapes through the bleed area.
A background that stops at the finished edge can leave a thin white line when the cutting position varies slightly.
Keep your name, phone number, logo and other important content comfortably inside the margins.
These items should not extend into the area that will be cut away.
For example, if a blue rectangle forms the card background, enlarge that rectangle.
Do not enlarge the entire design because that also pushes the text closer to the edge.
When both sides are ready, select Share and Download.
Choose PDF, use the Print Preset and open Advanced Settings.
Enable crop marks and bleed if your printer requests them, then download the file.
Here's the important distinction.
Bleed guides help you design, while crop marks in the exported PDF show where the card should be trimmed.
You do not need to draw either one manually.
Open the PDF and inspect both sides at a readable zoom.
Check the backgrounds, spelling and text spacing, then confirm the printer accepts Canva's fixed bleed settings
before sending the file.
