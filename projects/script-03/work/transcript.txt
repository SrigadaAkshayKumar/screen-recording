This tutorial shows you how to add an existing voiceover recording to a Canva video and synchronize it with your scenes.
Open your project, select Uploads and click Upload Files.
Choose your narration file, such as an MP3, M4A or WAV recording, and wait for it to finish uploading.
Find the recording in the Audio section of Uploads and add it to your design.
An audio clip appears in the timeline below the visuals.
Drag the clip so it's beginning lines up with the first scene you want to narrate.
If the recording starts with silence, trim that section before aligning it.
Play the project from the beginning.
Shorten or lengthen the scenes so the visuals change when the narration moves to the next idea.
If your narration is longer than the visuals, add another scene or extend the final scene to cover the remaining speech.
Select the original video clips and reduce their volume when you want only the new narration to be heard.
Then select the voiceover track and adjust its volume if necessary.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Uploading a recording only adds it to your media collection.
You still need to place it on the timeline and the visible timeline needs to cover every part of the narration you want to export.
Check the last sentence carefully so it is not cut off.
Download the finished project as an MP4 video and listen to the exported file.
