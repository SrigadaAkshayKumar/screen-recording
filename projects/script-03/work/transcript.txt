This tutorial shows you how to add a working RSVP form to a Canva invitation
and collect replies through a shared Canva link.
Open your invitation design.
In Elements, look under Browse categories for forms.
Choose a suitable form preset and add it to the page.
If this category is not available in your account,
use the linked form alternative shown at the end.
Select the form and click Edit.
In the Questions tab, change the first question to
Will you attend?
Add Yes and No as the answer choices and make the question required.
Add another question for the number of guests
using the question types offered in your form.
Keep the form short so guests can reply quickly.
Open Settings and enable respondent name collection
where it is available.
For this example, use a shared invitation design
rather than a website
because Canva documents different name collection behavior
for website forms.
Change the Submit button text to Send RSVP.
Add your reply deadline above the form
and adjust the colours to match the invitation.
Share a view-only Canva link.
Open it in a separate browser window and submit a test response.
Back in the editor, select the form and open responses.
Confirm that you can identify the guest
and read their attendance answer before sending the link out.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
The form cannot collect replies from a downloaded PDF, PNG or video.
Guests need to open the interactive Canva link.
If forms is unavailable,
create your RSVP form in Google Forms,
copy its responder link,
select an RSVP button in Canva and add that link.
Test the button in the shared design
or an unflattened PDF before distributing it.
