This tutorial shows you how to apply for Canva education using your teaching credentials.
First, check Canva's current eligibility guidelines.
The free teacher program primarily serves eligible, currently active primary and secondary educators at recognised schools.
University teaching or simply having a student email does not automatically qualify you for this program.
Go to canva.com.edu and choose the teacher verification option.
You can also open canva.com.edu sign up directly.
Sign into the Canva account you want to use for school.
If you already have an account containing your teaching materials, use that account so the application is connected to the correct profile.
Follow the prompts, identify yourself as a teacher and enter your school and teaching information.
Use your school email when possible.
Canva may recognise a verified school email domain and grant access immediately.
If further verification is requested, upload an accepted document showing your name, school and current teaching role.
Depending on your location, this may be a school ID, teaching credential or proof of employment.
Submit the application and check your email and Canva account for the result or any request for additional information.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A school email address and an approved education workspace are different things.
If your school already has a Canva team, you may still need to complete verification and join that team through an invitation or the options Canva provides after approval.
Once approved, switch into your education workspace and confirm that the teaching tools and resources are available before inviting students or organising class materials.
