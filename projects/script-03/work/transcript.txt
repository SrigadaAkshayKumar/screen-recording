This tutorial shows you how to turn a PDF into presentation slides using Canva's PDF to PowerPoint Converter.
Open Canva's PDF to PPT Converter in your browser.
Choose Upload Your File and select the PDF from your computer.
Sign in if Canva asks you to.
Wait for the conversion to finish.
Each PDF page becomes a separate slide, so check the page order before continuing.
Choose Edit to open the converted presentation in Canva.
Click through the slides and inspect the headings, images and tables.
Replace any substituted fonts and fix text that has shifted outside its original position.
If the PDF pages were already landscape slides, the converted layout may need only small adjustments.
If they were portrait document pages, reorganise the content for a presentation
instead of squeezing an entire page of small text onto the screen.
For example, turn a long paragraph into a short heading and three key points.
Move the remaining detail into another slide so the audience can read it comfortably.
When you are finished, use Canva's presentation controls to present the deck
or download the converted file as a PowerPoint presentation.
Open the downloaded file to check its layout in PowerPoint as well.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A scanned PDF can arrive as a flat image.
Converting that page into a slide does not automatically make every word editable.
Recreate the text with Canva text boxes when you need to rewrite it.
Check several slides against the original PDF before sharing,
especially pages containing tables or unusual fonts.
Your PDF is now a slide deck that you can review, customise and present.
