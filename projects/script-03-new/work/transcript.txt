This tutorial shows you a reliable work-around for using genuinely blurred video footage in a Canva project.
Canva's regular photo blur tool does not process moving video.
For this method we will prepare a blurred MP4 in CapCut's online editor and then finish the design in Canva.
Upload your original clip to CapCut and add it to the timeline.
Open Effects, choose Video Effects and browse the Blur category.
Apply a basic blur effect, then adjust its strength until the footage has the amount of softness you want.
Extend the effect strip from the beginning to the end of the clip.
Play the footage to check that the effect covers the whole video, including the final frames.
Export the result as an MP4.
Keep the original dimensions when possible so you can still crop and resize it in Canva.
Return to Canva, open uploads and upload the blurred MP4.
Add it to your video design and adjust its size, position and duration.
You can now place sharp text, graphics or a logo above the blurred footage.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Lowering a video's transparency changes, how see-through it is.
It does not blur the moving picture.
Use a clip with the blur actually rendered into it.
Preview your Canva design and download the finished result as an MP4 video.
