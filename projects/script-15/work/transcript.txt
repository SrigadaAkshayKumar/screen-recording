This tutorial shows you how to create a short branded outro that you can add to the end of your videos.
Create a video design using the same aspect ratio as the videos that will use the outro.
For a regular YouTube video, choose a landscape layout.
Search the template panel for video outro or start with a blank scene.
Set the background to one of your brand colours.
Upload your logo and place it where it has clear space around it.
Add your brand name or a short closing message.
Then add one main call to action, such as watch the next video or visit our website.
Use your brand's fonts and colours.
If your account has a brand kit, apply those assets from the brand panel.
Otherwise, choose the fonts and colours manually.
Give the outro a short duration, such as 8 seconds.
Animate the logo first, then make the call to action appear slightly later, using the text element's timeline bar.
Add a short music ending or sound effect if it fits your brand.
Lower the volume so it does not jump sharply above the main video's audio.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A subscribe button or video placeholder drawn in Canva becomes part of the exported picture.
It does not become clickable.
For a YouTube end screen, add the actual interactive elements in YouTube Studio after uploading.
Preview the outro and leave the final message visible long enough to read.
Download it as an MP4 and keep the editable Canva design so you can update the message or colours for future videos.
