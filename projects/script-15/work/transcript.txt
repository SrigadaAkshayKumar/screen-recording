This tutorial shows you how to create a short motion graphic in Canva using animated text, moving shapes, and controlled timing.
Create a blank landscape video design on Canva Desktop.
Add a blank background to the main timeline and extend it to about 6 seconds.
Choose a background colour, then open text, and add a short headline.
For this example, use three words that fit comfortably on one line.
Open elements and add a circle or another simple graphic.
Change its colour and place it beside the headline.
Add a smaller text box for a final message, such as start today.
Select the headline and click animate.
Choose a simple entrance animation, such as rise and preview the result.
Adjust its available speed or duration controls so the words are easy to read.
Select the circle, open animate, and choose create an animation.
Drag the circle along the path you want it to follow.
Adjust the movement controls, then save the path.
In the video timeline, position the headline so it starts at the beginning.
Move the circle's clip to start slightly later, then place the final message near the end.
Drag each element's left and right handles to control when it appears and disappears.
Here's the part many tutorials skip, and element's timeline length controls how long it is present,
while animate controls how it moves.
If the motion finishes too quickly, adjust the animation settings and preview again.
Play the whole sequence.
Leave the final message visible long enough to read and remove any motion that distracts from it.
Choose share, download, and mp4 video.
Open the downloaded file to confirm the movement and timing before using it in your project.
