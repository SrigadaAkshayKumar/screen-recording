This tutorial shows you how to give all generated captions an animated style in Canva
without choosing an effect for every line separately.
Open your video and select any generated caption segment on the timeline.
From the toolbar, choose Captions to open the Captions panel.
Switch to the Styles tab.
Browse the caption style packs and preview their movement before selecting one.
Look for a style that matches the pace of your video and keeps the words easy to read.
Select the style you want.
Canva applies that style pack to all captions in the video,
including the animation included with the pack.
Play the beginning, middle and end of the video.
Check a short caption and a longer one so you can see how the chosen style handles both.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A caption style pack combines animation with other appearance settings,
including fonts and colours.
Review the whole look after applying it,
especially if you previously customised individual captions.
This workflow is for generated captions.
Separate text boxes you typed manually are ordinary text elements
and are not automatically included in the caption style pack.
If Styles is unavailable in your editor,
use the individual captions' animate controls instead.
Download as an MP4 video and check several captions in the save file to confirm the result.
