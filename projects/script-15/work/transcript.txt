This tutorial shows you how to rename a Canva design and an uploaded media file so they are easier to find.
To rename a design, open it in the editor.
Click its current title in the top toolbar, type the new name and press Enter.
Use a descriptive name such as Canva Upload Tutorial Thumbnail instead of leaving the design as untitled.
You can also rename a design from the home page.
Open Projects, find the design and click its title beside the pencil icon.
Enter the new name and press Enter.
For an uploaded image or video, open Projects and go to Uploads or the folder containing that file.
Open its preview and use the Rename action in the file controls.
Enter the new name and confirm the change.
Here's the part Many Tutorials Skip.
A design title and the name of an uploaded asset are separate.
Renaming your thumbnail design does not rename the photo you uploaded into it,
and changing the Canva name does not rename the source file on your computer.
For repeat projects, include the topic and a clear version label such as Draft01 or Final.
This makes the correct file easier to recognize when several designs look similar.
Return to Projects and check that the new name is displayed.
Your design or uploaded file now has a clearer name in Canva.
