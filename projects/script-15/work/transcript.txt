This tutorial shows you how to change the length of scenes and clips in Canva's video timeline.
Open your video design and make sure the timeline is visible.
If you're in page mode, use the page thumbnail's pencil control to switch into the video timeline.
Select the clip or scene you want to adjust.
Zoom into the timeline so its start and end are easy to see.
To shorten a video clip, drag its right edge toward the left.
To restore more of the original footage, drag the edge outward as long as unused source footage is available.
Dragging the left edge changes where that section begins.
Preview the result so you do not remove the start of an important action or sentence.
For a scene that needs an exact duration, select the scene and use the clock control in the floating toolbar.
Enter the duration in seconds.
You can also select several scenes and use that timing control to give them the same duration.
If a title disappears too early, select its own timeline strip and extend the appropriate edge.
The title has separate timing from the scene beneath it.
Here's the key distinction.
Timeline zoom only changes how the timeline looks on screen.
It does not make the finished video longer or shorter.
Likewise, moving a clip changes its position while trimming its edges changes the portion that plays.
Check the final scene, narration and music after adjusting durations.
Then preview from the previous cut to confirm that the new timing feels right before downloading the video.
