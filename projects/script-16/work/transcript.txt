This tutorial shows you how to use Magic Animate to add coordinated animations to a Canva design.
Open a presentation, social media design or video in Canva.
Finish the main layout first so your text, photos and shapes are in their intended positions.
Click an empty area of the page and open Animate in the toolbar.
Find Magic Animate in the Animation panel.
Magic Animate is a premium feature.
If it is unavailable, check your plan access and make sure you are working in a supported design type.
Open Magic Animate and review the recommended style.
Choose it to apply the suggested combination of animations and transitions across your design.
You can also try one of the alternative styles.
Preview the entire design rather than checking only the first page.
Watch how headings enter, how images move and how one page connects to the next.
If a particular object needs a different treatment, select it and adjust its animation manually.
Use the object's timing controls when you need to change when it appears.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Automatic animation choices still need a readability check.
A good-looking entrance can leave a long sentence on screen for too little time,
so adjust the timing after applying the style.
For a live presentation, test the result in Present
To share a fixed animated version, click Share, Download and choose MP4 Video.
The design now has a coordinated animation style that you can refine.
