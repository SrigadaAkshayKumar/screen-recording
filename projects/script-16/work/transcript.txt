This tutorial shows you how to design a business card in Canva and prepare the front and back for printing.
Search Canva for business card and choose a template in the dimensions your printer uses.
Confirm the measurements before customizing because business card sizes vary.
Replace the sample business name and upload your logo.
On the front, keep the layout simple.
Your name, role and the most useful contact details.
Use a readable font for the phone number, email address and website.
Avoid adding every social account if it makes the text too small.
Create a second page for the back.
You can use it for a larger logo, a short description of your service or a QR code that opens your website.
To add a fixed link QR code, open apps.
Search for QR code and choose the static option.
Enter the final website address and save the code to your design.
Leave clear space around it and keep the contrast strong.
Keep important text and the QR code away from the trim edge.
Extend an edge-to-edge background into the bleed area required by your printer.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Print a rough copy at actual size and scan the QR code from that paper.
A code that works when enlarged on a monitor may become difficult to scan after it is reduced to card size.
Click share, download and choose PDF print.
Export the front and back in the correct order using the printer's requested crop mark and bleed settings.
For a professional print order, supply one card side per PDF page
unless the printer specifically requests a sheet of multiple cards.
Your business card file is now ready for proofing.
