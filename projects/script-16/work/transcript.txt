This tutorial shows you how to remove uploaded files from Canva, including several files at once.
Open a design and select Uploads in the left sidebar.
Hover over the file you want to remove, click its three-dot menu, and choose Move to Trash.
To manage uploads from the home page instead, open Projects, then All Projects, and enter the Uploads folder.
Find the file, open its three-dot menu, and choose Move to Trash.
For several files, hover over each thumbnail and tick its checkbox.
After selecting the correct files, click the trash icon in the action bar and confirm if Canva asks.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Deleting an image from a design page and deleting it from uploads are different actions.
Pressing Delete while the image is selected on your page removes that placement.
It does not clean the uploaded file out of your account.
If you moved the wrong file to trash, open Projects, then Trash, select the item,
and choose Restore while it is still available.
Only choose permanent deletion or empty trash when you are finished reviewing the items.
Media already used in an existing design can remain part of that design after the upload is removed.
Return to uploads and check that the unused files no longer appear in your collection.
