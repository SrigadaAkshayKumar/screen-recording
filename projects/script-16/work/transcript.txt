This tutorial shows you how to organise your logo, colours and fonts in a Canva brand kit.
From the Canva homepage, open Brand.
Open the Brand Kit dropdown.
Choose Create New Brand Kit, enter your brand name and select Create.
Start with Logos.
Choose Add to Category, select Upload Brand Assets and upload the logo files you use regularly.
Include a main logo and if you have them, light and dark versions.
Clear file names make it easier to choose the right one later.
Next, open Colours.
Add a palette and enter your brand's colour values.
Use exact hex codes rather than trying to match each colour by eye.
For a simple example, save your main blue, a dark text colour and a light background colour.
Name the palette so it is easy to recognise.
Open fonts and set the heading and body styles.
Choose the typefaces, weights and sizes you want to use, then save the settings.
If your plan supports custom font uploads, add the licensed font files you need before setting those styles.
Now open a design and select Brand from the editor's side panel.
Choose your kit, add a logo and use its colours and text styles in the layout.
Here's the important limitation.
Available brand kit features depend on your plan and role.
In a team, you may need an administrator or brand designer to upload assets or change settings.
Setting up the kit also does not automatically restyle every older design.
Open a design and apply the saved assets where needed.
