This tutorial shows you how to create a real cover in Canva that stays readable when it
appears on your Instagram profile.
From Canva's homepage, search for Instagram real cover.
Choose a portrait template or create a blank design measuring 1080 by 1920 pixels.
Upload a photo or a clear frame from your video and use it as the background.
Pick an image that explains the topic without requiring someone to read a long headline.
Add a short title in a large readable font.
Use a strong contrast between the words and the background.
If the image is busy, place a solid shape behind the text or move the headline into a quieter area.
Keep the main title and your subject toward the centre of the design.
Leave extra space near the top, bottom and sides so the cover can survive different preview crops.
Add a small logo or a consistent brand detail if you want your real covers to look connected.
Duplicate this design for future covers and change the photo and headline.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
A cover can look perfect at full height and still lose words in the profile grid.
Check the design at a small size and review the actual cover crop inside Instagram before publishing.
Select Share, Download and PNG or JPG.
Save the finished cover to the device you will use for uploading.
During the reel's upload process, open the cover options.
Select your saved image and adjust the available previews.
Make sure the title and subject remain visible before sharing the reel.
