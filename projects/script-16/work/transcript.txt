This tutorial shows you how to make text scroll horizontally across a Canva video using a custom motion path.
Create a blank landscape video design and add a background.
Set up an eight second section for this example.
Open text and add your message in one text box.
Use a readable font and widen the box enough to keep a short message on one line.
Place it near the bottom of the canvas.
Add a contrasting rectangle behind the text if the background makes the words difficult to read.
Keep the rectangle still so it acts as a clear strip for the scrolling message.
Move the text toward the right edge, leaving a small part on the page so you can still select it.
Click animate then, create an animation.
Hold shift while dragging the text horizontally to the left.
Continue until the last letter has moved beyond the left edge, then release to finish recording the path.
Use the movement controls to adjust the animation speed.
Preview it and check that the entire message enters, crosses the frame and leaves without stopping halfway.
If the path is uneven, delete it and record a straight path again.
In the video timeline, make sure the text element stays active for the entire scroll.
Extend the background to cover the same period.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Extending the background does not automatically slow the text's motion.
Adjust the path speed as well and check that the text clip is long enough for the movement to finish.
Play the result at normal speed.
If the words are difficult to follow, shorten the message or slow the animation.
Export through share, download and MP4 video to preserve the scrolling movement.
