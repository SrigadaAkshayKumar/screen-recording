This tutorial shows you how to remove the automatic captions you generated inside a Canva video project.
Open the original Canva project and pause where a caption is visible.
Select a generated caption, then open Captions from the toolbar.
Choose Delete All Captions in the Captions panel to remove the generated captions across the design.
If you only want to remove one line, use the Delete control beside that line instead.
Play through the project after deleting.
Look at sections from every part of the video to make sure you remove the captions you intended and left other titles intact.
Here's the part many tutorials skip.
Canva can remove captions that exist as editable elements in the project.
If you uploaded a finished video with subtitles and already burned into its picture,
those words are part of the footage and cannot be deleted through the captions panel.
For burned-in subtitles, use the original video without captions or return to the project that created them.
Cropping may hide words near an edge, but it also removes part of the picture.
If captions only appear during playback and have no editable layer, check whether view-only accessibility captions are enabled.
Download a new MP4 after deleting the editable captions.
An older exported file will still contain the subtitles from before the change.
